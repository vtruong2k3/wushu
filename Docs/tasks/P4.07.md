## 1. Analysis

**Goal:** the last step of the quality layer (W6). Six stages exist and each works alone: the mask (P4.01), the swap flag (P4.06), Hampel (P4.03), the gap layer (P4.02), the uniform grid (P4.04) and the Butterworth filter (P4.05). The earlier tasks left
to this one four things: (1) **wiring them in the order that DEC-0046 and DEC-0049 fixed** (mask; swap flag and Hampel, both on the mask layer; gap layer with the swap mask as `blocked`; grid; filter); (2) the **coverage** that W6 asks for, "by joint, by
segment and by reason of exclusion", the second denominator of section 8.3 of the v3 plan ("the share of frames with a pose of enough quality in the eligible stretches"); (3) **`quality.json`**, the file that carries it, with the warnings of the run
(`lr_swap_suspect@12.40-12.73`) and the parameters that each stage used; (4) two **decisions** that the stages passed on: which lattice the filtered layer lives on (DEC-0047, Q5) and how the Hampel floor of 2 px scales with the size of the person (DEC-0046).
Nothing here changes a value of any layer: the chain only calls the stages, and the coverage only counts.

**What the analysis measured (numbers only, nothing committed; D20 pass of P3.08, N = 1, 22 chunks, 158,326 rows; the chain wired as proposed):**

- **The stages compose.** Mask, swap flag (on the mask layer, torso of the layer), Hampel (on the mask layer), gap layer (`blocked` = the swap mask), grid (the identity on all 22 chunks) and filter run one after the other with no adaptation; the whole chain takes
  **4.8 s for 158,326 rows (0.31 s per 10,000 rows)**, so about 3.5 s for an hour of video at 30 fps.
- **The numbers that the coverage will state** (rows of the segments, the 1 s margins left out: 157,366 rows, 4,091,516 cells; Hampel floor of 2 px): **54.8 % of the rows are tracked** (and every tracked row is a usable frame: no frame is `frame_invalid`); of the cells
  **54.53 % are observed, 0.262 % bridged and 45.21 % missing**; of all cells the reasons for not being observed are `not_tracked` 45.16 %, `outlier` 0.27 % and `low_conf` 0.04 % (bridged cells included, because a bridged cell keeps its reason); 54.78 % of the cells carry
  a filtered value and 585 usable cells (0.014 %) are in runs too short to be filtered; the swap flag marks 0.011 % of the cells as `suspect` and 0.021 % as `blocked`. The cells that stayed NaN, by the outcome of their gap: `open_start` 1,126,358 and `open_end` 710,124
  (a loss of the target at the start or the end of a chunk: no anchor), `never_fill` 11,993 (a loss between two stretches), `too_long` 915, `blocked` 408. **Inside the tracked rows 99.90 % of the cells are usable.** By video the usable share of the cells is
  62.2 % (`d20-v1`), 61.7 %, 43.9 % and 54.8 % (`d20-v4`): it is the share of the time that the performer is in the picture, a property of the footage (cuts and close-ups: 38 % to 56 % of the frames of a web video are not the target's), not of the pose model.
  By joint the usable share over the segment rows is 54.6 % (left wrist) to 54.8 % (head).
- **The Hampel floor as a fixed 2 px or as 1 % of the torso.** The torso of the chunks is 141 to 307 px (median 184), so 1 % of it is a floor of **1.41 to 3.07 px (median 1.84)**. With the relative floor the outlier cells are **0.188 %** of the cells against **0.271 %** with 2 px, the
  bridged cells 0.180 % against 0.262 %, the gaps refused as `too_long` 878 against 915; the usable share inside the tracked rows is 99.91 % against 99.90 %. The difference is small on this footage, in either direction by chunk (a chunk with a small torso gets a lower floor and more flags, one with a large
  torso the reverse); the relative floor is the same 2 px at the 197 px torso on which the 2 px was chosen (DEC-0046), and it does not depend on the resolution or the distance of the camera.
- **What exists to write the file.** `schemas/_base.py` has the manifest base (Pydantic v2, `extra="forbid"`, frozen, floats without NaN), `write_manifest` and `read_manifest` (atomic, strict, errors that name the field) and `store/artifacts.py` the atomic writers: `quality.json` needs no new machinery.

**What is not obvious, and why this plan asks questions:**

- **"Coverage" has to say what its denominator is.** A cell can be observed, bridged or missing; a row can be tracked or not; a missing cell has a reason and, if it was in a gap, an outcome. The report states **counts** with the **rows** and **cells** of the interval next to them,
  so that any ratio (and the three ratios of section 8.3 per metric and camera, which are P10.04's) is a division of two numbers in the file. The shares above are not stored.
- **The lattice.** The filter works on the regular grid of P4.04. On a clip with a constant frame rate the grid *is* the rows and the question does not exist (all of D20). On a variable-frame-rate clip the filtered layer on the **grid** has no loss at the edges of holes and a regular clock (time =
  rows x step), while the same layer on the **source rows** keeps the original timestamps but loses about 0.07 % of the usable cells and weighs rows by unequal intervals. The coverage is a share of **time**, so it is counted on the grid.
- **Segments.** A run is one segment of a take (`run_manifest.segment_id`); the 1 s margins that the pose stage reads on both sides are in the layers (the filters need them) but not in the coverage. "By segment" is the segment itself and any named interval of the same take that the caller passes, such as the stretches that
  the coach asked to correct (P10.04 reports the coverage on them).
- **The report is not a threshold.** No coverage is judged here: "too low" is a limit of a metric (W6 table: "coverage under the metric's threshold" goes back to W3), and AGENTS.md 5.5 forbids a universal one.

**The rules** (every rule has a test sub-ID below):

1. **The chain, in this order.** (a) `mask_track` / `apply_mask`; (b) the torso of the mask layer (`torso_length_px`); (c) the swap flag on the mask layer with that torso; (d) Hampel on the mask layer with a floor of `hampel_floor_fraction x torso` pixels (or the floor of `hampel` as it is if the
   fraction is None **or the torso cannot be measured**, which is then a warning); (e) `interpolate_gaps` on the Hampel layer with `blocked` = the swap mask; (f) `resample_gaps`; (g) `lowpass_grid`. Every stage reads what the order gives it and the result of a stage is never fed back. A `QualityError` of a stage is re-raised
   naming the stage.
2. **The lattice of the report is the grid.** A cell of the report is a cell of the grid layer (`GridLayer`, identity for a uniform clock): its class, its reason and its flags are the grid's. The time of a row is `step_s`, so `duration_s` = rows x `step_s`. The report states `resampled` and, if it is true, `mixed_rows`.
3. **Intervals.** The first interval is the **segment**: the grid rows whose time is within the first and the last row with `in_segment` (closed at both ends, +1e-9 s); the margins are not in it. Every named interval is the grid rows with `start <= t < end`. Names are distinct, `start < end`; an interval that holds no row is reported with zero counts.
4. **The classes of a cell** (disjoint, they add up to `cells` = rows x 26): **observed** (reason `ok`; the value is a measurement, or a mix of two when the row is `mixed`), **bridged** (`interpolated`: a short gap that the gap layer filled, or a cell built on one) and **missing** (NaN). `unobserved_by_reason` counts all the cells whose reason is not `ok`
   (bridged cells included, because they keep it), `missing_by_reason` the cells that are NaN, one count for each of the seven names of `REASON_NAMES` except `ok`. The sums hold: observed + bridged + missing = cells; the counts of `missing_by_reason` add up to `missing`; those of `unobserved_by_reason` to bridged + missing.
5. **The frames.** A grid row is a **usable frame** if at least `min_essential_fraction` (0.5, the mask's) of the essential joints have a value in the final layer; a row is **not tracked** if all 26 cells have the reason `not_tracked`. (A row can be neither: a tracked row with few joints.)
6. **The gaps.** The gap table of P4.02 is on the source rows; for each interval the report counts the gaps whose first row lies in it, and their cells, by outcome (`filled`, `too_long`, `never_fill`, `blocked`, `open_start`, `open_end`, `open_both`). On a uniform clock these are the rows of the grid.
7. **The flags.** `filtered` and `unfiltered` count the usable cells with and without a value from the filter (`unfiltered` includes the runs that are too short and a clip too slow for the filter); `suspect` and `blocked` the cells of the swap flag; per joint likewise. A cell can be both observed and `blocked`: the swap flag does not move it out of its class.
8. **Warnings** (strings, sorted by the first row they refer to, none for the margins): `lr_swap_suspect@12.40-12.73` for each maximal run of rows of the segment with a `blocked` cell (the times of its first and last row, seconds with two decimals; one row: `@12.40`); `grid_resampled` if the timeline was not uniform; `filter_skipped_above_nyquist` if the
   filter could not be designed; `torso_unmeasured` if there was no torso (no swap flag, the fixed floor).
9. **Nothing is changed.** No layer is altered by the coverage; the report holds counts, times, names of joints and of reasons and the parameters, nothing else. Counts are integers; times are finite floats.
10. **The file.** `save_quality(path, report)` writes `quality.json` atomically and `load_quality(path)` reads it strictly: an unknown or missing field, a wrong type, a NaN, or a `schema_version` other than `quality/1` is an error that names the field; the same input gives the same bytes.

**Constraints that apply** (AGENTS.md 5): 5.1 no fabricated data: the chain only calls the stages, the report only counts, a cell is never moved from missing to observed; 5.2 real timestamps: the report's times are the grid's (real PTS); 5.3 layers apart: every layer is kept in the result, none is altered; 5.4 units documented (seconds, counts, torso lengths); **5.5** no universal threshold: the report judges no coverage; the Hampel
floor as a share of the torso is a data-validity parameter and its value is the one chosen on D20; 5.8 atomic writes, the file written last; 5.9 privacy: the report holds no value of a keypoint, no name and no path (a test reads the JSON for them); section 6: Pydantic v2 with `schema_version`, type hints, docstrings with units, `QualityError` and `ManifestError`, no bare `except`.

**Unknowns, risks, questions** (the maintainer is asked; the proposed answer is applied if there is no other):

- **Q1. Is the chain part of this task?** The plan row says coverage and `quality.json`; DEC-0045, DEC-0046, DEC-0049 and the tasks P4.01 to P4.06 each say that **P4.07 wires the stages**. **Proposed: yes** (`quality/chain.py`, `run_quality`): without it nothing runs the stages in the decided order, the coverage has nothing to count, and P7 would have to decide the
  order. Alternative: leave the wiring to P7 and count on layers passed in (the order would then be written in a worker that does not exist yet).
- **Q2. The lattice.** **Proposed: the filtered layer lives on the grid** and the coverage is counted on it; `filtered_on_source()` gives the values on the source rows for the overlay of the interface, with its loss at hole edges (none on a uniform clock, 0.07 % on a rate 1 % off). Identical for every clip that exists. Alternative: the source rows (the original timestamps, unequal weights,
  the loss); the grid stays a detour of the filter.
- **Q3. The Hampel floor.** **Proposed: 1 % of the torso of the layer** (`hampel_floor_fraction = 0.01`; 2 px at the 197 px torso of M-QLT-03), the fixed 2 px as the fallback when the torso cannot be measured. On D20 it is 1.41 to 3.07 px by chunk and flags 0.188 % of the cells against 0.271 %. Alternative: the fixed 2 px (the measured value, but a number of pixels that means
  something else at another resolution or distance).
- **Q4. The shape of the coverage.** **Proposed: counts with explicit denominators** (rows, cells), three disjoint classes (observed, bridged, missing), the reasons twice (all unobserved, and the missing), the gap outcomes, the frames, the flags, per joint and per interval (rules 3 to 7); no fraction is stored. Alternative: fractions as well (redundant, and a place for a rounding to differ from a division).
- **Q5. Does this task write `quality.json`?** **Proposed: yes**, schema `quality/1`, with `write_manifest` and `read_manifest` (about thirty lines, the helpers exist); X.29 keeps `pose_masked.npz` and `pose_filtered.npz` (its row is updated). Alternative: only the in-memory `QualityReport`, the file with X.29 (two tasks would then own one folder of files).
- **Q6. The warnings.** **Proposed:** the four of rule 8, in the form of the manifest example of TECH_WORKFLOW (`lr_swap_suspect@12.40-12.73`). The text of a report for the coach (P8) is built from them, not here.
- **Q7. What a bridged cell is in the coverage.** **Proposed: a class of its own**, neither observed nor missing; usable like an observed one, counted apart so that the share of measured cells stays visible (0.262 % of D20). A cell of a `mixed` row counts in the class of its parents and `mixed_rows` says how many rows are not measurements.
- **Risk: the grid and the source rows.** On a non-uniform clock the gap table and the reasons live on different lattices; the report counts gaps on the source rows (rule 6) and everything else on the grid, and says which. The tests build both clocks (a dropped frame, the step-down of SYN-07) and check the sums.
- **Risk: the sums.** Every count must add up (rule 4); the tests check the partition on 200 random chains against a slow cell-by-cell count, and a hand-counted fixture.
- **Risk: a file that is read by the next stages for years.** The schema is versioned and strict; a field that is added later bumps `quality/1`; the report is small (26 joints per interval).
- **Risk: scope.** About 1.25 days; if the schema or the chain grows, the file (`schemas/quality.py`, `quality_io.py`) can be split off as P4.07b (nothing else depends on it before P7).

## 2. Plan

**Design:**

```python
# quality/chain.py
@dataclass(frozen=True)
class QualityParams:
    mask: MaskParams = MaskParams(); swap: SwapParams = SwapParams(); hampel: HampelParams = HampelParams()
    hampel_floor_fraction: float | None = 0.01      # share of the torso, or None: hampel.scale_floor_px as it is
    gaps: GapParams = GapParams(); resample: ResampleParams = ResampleParams(); filter: ButterworthParams = ButterworthParams()

@dataclass(frozen=True, eq=False)
class QualityResult: mask, swap, hampel, gaps, grid, filtered, torso_px, params, report;  def filtered_on_source(self) -> np.ndarray

def run_quality_arrays(keypoints, scores, t_sec, *, tracked=None, in_segment=None, params=None, intervals=None) -> QualityResult
def run_quality(track: TargetTrack, params=None, intervals=None) -> QualityResult

# quality/coverage.py
def compute_coverage(grid, gaps, filtered, swap, mask, in_segment, params, intervals) -> QualityReport   # pure counting

# schemas/quality.py: QualityReport, IntervalCoverage, JointCoverage, GapCounts, ReasonCounts, Timeline  (schema "quality/1")
# quality/quality_io.py: save_quality(path, report), load_quality(path)
```

Data flow: validate (`intervals`: names, order) → `mask_track` or `apply_mask` → torso → swap flag → Hampel with the floor → gap layer with `blocked` → grid → filter → `compute_coverage` over the grid lattice (the segment from `in_segment` mapped to the grid by time, the named intervals by time) with boolean masks and `bincount`s, no loop over cells → the report →
`QualityResult`. `save_quality` and `load_quality` are the manifest helpers with the model.

## 3. Plan re-read (before coding)

Plan re-read: 2026-11-17 — OK, with the corrections below. The maintainer confirmed Q1 to Q7 as proposed (2026-11-17). The whole file was re-read against the current code (`mask.py`, `filters.py`, `gaps.py`, `resample.py`, `lr_swap.py`,
`schemas/_base.py`, `schemas/track.py`, `store/artifacts.py`, `track/track_io.py`, `tests/helpers/synth.py`, `track_build.py`, the contract test of P4.06). Nothing in the six stages changed since the analysis. The refinements, all inside the confirmed design:

1. **Reasons are six names, not seven.** `REASON_NAMES` has seven entries with `ok` first; the two reason tables hold the six others (`low_conf`, `non_finite`, `not_tracked`, `frame_invalid`, `outlier`, `no_frame`), as fixed fields of a model; a test ties the field names to `REASON_NAMES[1:]` so that a new reason code breaks the build and bumps `quality/1`.
2. **Gap counts by the cells inside the interval (rule 6).** Rule 6 said "the gaps whose first row lies in the interval". A gap that starts in the 1 s margin and goes on into the segment would then be lost from the segment although its cells are in it. The report counts, for each interval, **the cells of each gap that lie in the interval's source rows**, and counts a gap if at least one of its cells does. On a uniform clock this gives an identity that the tests check: the cells of `filled` gaps equal `bridged`, those of all other outcomes equal `missing`.
3. **Two lattices, stated.** Cells, reasons, frames and filter flags are counted on the **grid**; the gap table and the swap flags are on the **source rows** (the swap flag is a property of the mask layer, and mapping it onto the grid would invent semantics). `IntervalCoverage.source_rows` is the denominator of the second group; it equals `rows` for a uniform clock.
4. **Empty and one-row cases.** `t_start` and `t_end` of the segment are `None` when the clip has no row of the segment (an unknown is `None`, never 0); `duration_s` is `None` for exactly one row (no step is known), 0.0 for no rows; `timeline.step_s`, `sampling_hz` and `torso_px` are `None` when NaN.
5. **Intervals.** A named interval is `[start, end)` with a tolerance of 1e-9 s applied to both ends (a row 1 ulp off a boundary is on the boundary); the segment is the stretch from the first to the last row with `in_segment`, closed, as planned. Names match `^[A-Za-z0-9_.-]{1,64}$` (no free text in a file that is logged and shared) and `segment` is reserved.
6. **Warnings order.** `lr_swap_suspect@...` in time order first, then `torso_unmeasured`, `grid_resampled`, `filter_skipped_above_nyquist` in this fixed order. The swap warnings are computed on the **source rows** of the segment (the runs are clipped to it).
7. **`save_quality` returns the SHA-256 of the file** (like `save_track`: the run manifest of P7 lists it); `load_quality` is `read_manifest` with the model.
8. **Self-consistent file.** The model validators of `quality/1` check the partition and the sums (rule 4) so that a hand-edited file that disagrees with itself is refused on load; they are part of T-QLT-11l.
9. **Cells come from the final layer.** Classes, reasons and flags are read from the `FilterResult` (which carries `reason` and `interpolated` unchanged); the grid layer supplies `mixed` and the map.
