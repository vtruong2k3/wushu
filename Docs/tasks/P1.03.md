## 1. Analysis

**Goal:** one document that says what the coaches need, in order, and which of the three metrics stays, changes or goes,
with the evidence for each statement. It is the first condition of gate G00 and the input of P1.07 and P1.08.

**Agent role:** build `Docs/discovery/summary.md` from the notes with every claim pointing to the notes that support it;
count; draft the keep / change / drop table. O and H decide and sign.

**In scope:** top 3 problems; per metric a decision with the evidence; baseline times table; open issues; signatures.

**Out of scope:** metric formulas (P1.07); pilot targets (P1.08); new metrics beyond the three (a new metric is a scope
change: plan update plus a DEC).

**Proposed decision rule (to confirm, Q1):** a metric is kept when most of the interviewed coaches say they would use it
and it can be checked against a hand measurement later (P10.02) and is computable from the 2D views planned; it is
changed when coaches want a different quantity with the same data; it is dropped otherwise. With 3 to 5 interviews, the
document states the sample size and that no percentage is claimed.

**Top-3 ranking rule (proposal):** frequency across notes times minutes per occurrence from the baseline table.

**Constraints that apply:** AGENTS.md §5.12 (O and H sign: role and date, not names), §5.9 (pseudonyms), §5.5 (no
thresholds appear here).
