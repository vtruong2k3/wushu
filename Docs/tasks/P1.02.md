## 1. Analysis

**Goal:** at least 3 interview notes that record each coach's current workflow with minutes per step, pain points, and
a keep / change / drop reaction to each of the three metric sketches. This is the evidence for gate G00 ("a concrete need")
and the time baseline for the pilot targets (P1.08).

**Agent role:** prepare `Docs/discovery/notes/_TEMPLATE.md` before the first interview; after each interview check the
note for completeness and for personal data. The agent does not take part in interviews and does not choose coaches.

**In scope:** note template; interview scheduling checklist for O; completeness and privacy check of each note.

**Out of scope:** the guide (P1.01); the synthesis and any decision (P1.03); paying or contracting coaches (P1.08).

**Output:** `Docs/discovery/notes/<H-id>_<date>.md`, at least 3 (plan check), up to 5.

**Template fields:** pseudonym H-id, date, duration, years of teaching in a bracket, class size; the 24-form version
taught, its division into movements and the repetitions (input for P1.04); workflow steps with
minutes and tools; time to review one 6-minute take; ranked pain points; wishes before the sketches; per sketch: use,
what would make it wrong, number not trusted, keep / change / drop; camera and space constraints; hours per month
available; follow-up requests. No names, no contact data, no student names, no workplace name.

**Constraints that apply:** AGENTS.md §5.9 and §9.5 (pseudonyms only; the table H-id to person stays outside git, see
Q1); §5.12 (coach statements are input, the decision is H and O's in P1.03).

**Risks:** coach hours are an external dependency (plan §17 marks P1.02 as a waiting point). If fewer than 2 coaches are
available, gate G00 says to shrink the scope to a geometry-measurement proof of concept. The two coaches who will
co-design (H1, H2) may also be interviewed; their notes are flagged because their answers can be biased toward the design.
