## 1. Analysis

**Goal.** Appendix B3 makes this ablation **mandatory**: *"cut-off ablation of the filter (4/6/8 Hz) to measure the sensitivity of the number of pauses"*; the plan row adds the hip amplitude; P4.05 and DEC-0048 left the 6 Hz of W6 **untuned** on purpose ("P5.08 is where the value is judged"); v3 6.3 asks that "the filter record its parameters and check the effect on the pauses". The deliverable is the report `Docs/reports/p5_filter_ablation.md` with **numbers**: how the number and the length of the pauses (P5.07) and the hip amplitude (P5.03, P5.04) change when the final layer is made with a cut-off of 4, 6 or 8 Hz, on data with a known truth and on real data without one. It decides nothing by itself: the cut-off is a data-validity parameter of W6 and changing the default is the maintainer's decision (Q9).

**Five facts that shape the plan.**

1. **The pauses are hypersensitive to the cut-off and the hip amplitude is not.** On real data the paused share of a group moves by tens of percent between 4 and 8 Hz at one `(τ, d_min)` (D20 hips at `τ` 0.3: 69 % to 44 %), and the number of pauses of a file matches the 6 Hz count in about 10 % of the files; the hip amplitude moves by 0.2 % (Kinect, 111 files) to about 3 % (video chunks, measured on 2 chunks so far) of its value. The report has to say, in numbers, that `τ`, `d_min` and the cut-off are **one triple**: a `τ` learned from the coach's labels is valid for the filter that made the layer. This is an input to the signed `metric_record` (P5.09) and to the pipeline (P7): the record has to carry the cut-off, and the layer already does (`quality.json` and the layer's meta record `cutoff_hz`).
2. **A lower cut-off buys a lower floor and pays with smeared onsets.** 4 Hz lowers the speed floor of a still joint by 40 % against 6 Hz and finds a pause near the floor that 6 Hz loses (SYN-05 at 1 px); it also moves the boundary of a sharp onset by more than a frame (0.1 s ramp: 1.75 steps against 0.75). Nothing in the generator says how sharp the real onsets of a tai chi form are: that is for the coach and for the pilot, and the report must say so instead of choosing.
3. **A count is a poor sensitivity measure on real data.** Many short pauses flicker around `τ`; the counts of two cut-offs differ in almost every file while the paused rows agree at 70 to 95 %. The report therefore leads with the **paused share of the measured rows** and the **row-level agreement** (IoU) with the reference cut-off, and gives the count (sum, and the share of runs with the same count as 6 Hz) as the secondary number it is.
4. **The truth exists only on the generator; real data gives sensitivity, not accuracy.** SYN-01, SYN-02, SYN-05 and SYN-07 give the truth of the floor, the amplitude and the pauses; D20 (video pose of an instructor, web video, N = 1) and UMONS (Kinect, 13 techniques that are not the 24 forms) have no label of a pause and no hip height truth. They are reported **apart** (AGENTS.md 9.4.4) and as differences from the 6 Hz run, never as errors; the noise of the generator is white and Gaussian and the noise of a real pose is correlated in time, so a real floor is not the generator's.
5. **The filter changes values, not reasons.** The filter copies `reason` and `interpolated` cell by cell, so the coverage and the valid intervals of every metric must be the same at every cut-off; that is an invariant the tool checks on every run (a difference would be a bug of P4.05 and is registered), and the only thing a cut-off changes besides the values is which runs are too short to be filtered (`filtered = False`).

**Conventions fixed by this task** (each is a place where the report, P5.09 and the pilot could otherwise disagree):

1. **The conditions.** Cut-offs `4.0`, `6.0`, `8.0` Hz and a reference of **no filter** (`1000.0` Hz, which every grid of this project puts above Nyquist: the filter is skipped, flagged `above_nyquist`); the **reference** for every difference is **6.0 Hz** (the default of W6); order 4 and every other parameter are the chain's defaults. A run's four layers have the same grid, reasons and flags.
2. **The pauses** are `find_pauses` of P5.07 on `group_speed` of the final layer; `τ` and `d_min` are a **grid with no recommended cell**: on real data `τ` in {0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.5} Λ/s with `d_min` 0.3 s, and `τ` 0.2 Λ/s with `d_min` 0.5 s; on the generator `τ` as a share (10 %) of the true peak and as an absolute 0.3 Λ/s. The report states that these are cells of a sensitivity grid and not values of a record.
3. **The measures of agreement between a cut-off and 6 Hz** on one run: the **paused share** (paused rows over measured rows of that cut-off), the **row-level IoU** (paused in both over paused in either, over the rows measured in both), the **count** (and whether it equals the 6 Hz count) and the **boundary shift** of the pauses matched by overlap (the shift of the start and of the end, in steps of the grid). Aggregated over runs by summing numerators and denominators (a pooled IoU: a run with few rows does not weigh like a long one) and, for the counts and the shifts, by their distribution (median, p95, max).
4. **The hip amplitude** is `hip_height_range` of variant **B** on real data (A needs a reference standing segment that no chunk or file has) and of A and B on SYN-02; `stable_intervals` is the whole segment, an explicit convention of the ablation (nobody confirmed a stable height); the difference is **relative to the 6 Hz value** of the same run.
5. **Real data is a run per chunk or per file**, the segment is the whole run, and a run with no measured row for a metric is counted as such, not as a zero. D20 chunks use the target of `audit.analyse.target_track` (the P3.08 review rule: the most persistent large person), which gives a target in all 22 chunks; the default rule of the product fails on 4 of them (a title card or another shot at the start), and the report says which rule was used.
6. **Only numbers and the dataset's own ids** leave the tool: `d20-v1` to `d20-v4`, the UMONS sequence ids; no path, no image, no value of a file (AGENTS.md 5.9, 9.4).

**Constraints that apply (AGENTS.md section 5):** 1 no fabricated observation (every number is computed from a layer of the real chain; a run without a measured row is reported as such); 2 real timestamps (the grid of each run as the chain makes it); 3 layers stay apart (each cut-off is its own `PoseFiltered`, the raw and masked layers are not touched); 4 units documented (Λ/s, steps of the grid, percent of the 6 Hz value); **5 no universal threshold** (the `(τ, d_min)` grid is a sensitivity grid, the output is not a record); 9 nothing personal; datasets: **9.4.1** the registry rows of D20 and D12 are updated for this use before the run, **9.4.3** allowed use `dev-test`/`benchmark` for both, **9.4.4** UMONS (13 techniques) and D20 (instructor video, one performer) are reported apart and never as product accuracy, **9.4.7** the tests that need the data carry `dataset` and skip without it, **9.4.8** nothing under `datasets/` or `data/` goes to git; 11 no third-party code.

**Unknowns, risks, questions** (answered with the defaults below so that the work can follow; each can be reversed before the report is written):

- **Q1. The conditions.** Default: **4, 6, 8 Hz as B3 asks, plus no filter as a fourth reference**, 6 Hz the reference of every difference. Alternative: only the three (the effect of the filter itself, the 40 % to 50 % of the speed floor that it removes, would not be in the report).
- **Q2. Module and tool, or a scratch script.** Default: **a reusable `bench/filter_ablation.py` and a tool**, with tests on the generator and the three sources behind one command: the ablation will have to be re-run on the real segments of P5.10, on the pilot data (P9) and whenever the filter or a metric changes (AGENTS.md 9.2.4: a number is reproduced, not trusted), and P4.08 set the pattern. Cost: the task is 1 day and needs the maintainer's confirmation. Alternative: a scratch script outside the repository and a report of numbers (0.5 day, as P4.05's M-QLT-05 did): quicker, but not reproducible by the next person.
- **Q3. The data.** Default: the **generator** (truth), **D20** (22 chunks, video pose) and **UMONS** (111 files, Kinect), reported **apart**; D20 chunks by the audit's target rule (convention 5). Alternative: leave the Kinect out (it is not video pose and not the 24 forms; it is the only other real data and its sensor noise differs from video).
- **Q4. `τ` and `d_min`.** Default: the **grid** of convention 2, no recommended cell, and the share of the true peak on the generator. Alternative: choose a `τ` from the footage (the p25 of the speed of a group): it would be a threshold learned from unlabelled data.
- **Q5. What to lead with.** Default: the **paused share and the row-level IoU**, with the count as a secondary number (fact 3). Alternative: the count only (what B3 literally says; unstable on real data).
- **Q6. The hip amplitude.** Default: **B on real data, A and B on SYN-02**, difference from 6 Hz and error against the truth (convention 4). Alternative: add the amplitude of the profile's first harmonic or the hip's vertical speed (a number nobody asked for).
- **Q7. The generator's sweep.** Default: noise **0, 1 and 2 px**, seeds **1 to 5**, SYN-05 and SYN-07 (the variable frame rate), the **ramp** of the pauses 0.2, 0.1, 0.05 and 0.02 s (the sharpness of an onset), SYN-01 for the floor, SYN-02 for the amplitude. Alternative: noise 1 px only (the sharp-onset cost and the high-noise behaviour would be missing).
- **Q8. Invariants.** Default: the tool **fails loudly** if the reasons, the interpolated flags, the grid or the valid intervals differ between cut-offs of one run (fact 5); a failure is a bug to register, not a number to report.
- **Q9. The default cut-off.** Default: **no change in this task.** The report gives the numbers and a proposal with its trade-off (fact 2); changing `ButterworthParams.cutoff_hz` is the maintainer's decision, a separate task with a DEC-NNNN, new golden numbers for P4 and P5, and the plan of P5.09 (the record carries the cut-off). Alternative: change it here if the numbers are clear (it would move the golden numbers of the P4 and P5 tests and settle a question that the pilot has not asked yet).
- **Q10. Where the numbers go.** Default: `Docs/reports/p5_filter_ablation.md` in English (as the P3 and P4 reports), numbers and ids only; the tool writes its tables to `data/reports/p5_08/` (ignored by git), as P4.08 did.
- **Q11. Test IDs.** Default: **T-MET-12** (the machinery, unit), **T-MET-13** (the generator, golden), **M-MET-01** (the real-data run, manual, in the report). The plan has no ID for P5.08 ("Số liệu"); these are added to section 15.6 at the close.
- **Q12. The dataset registry.** Default: update the D20 and D12 rows (allowed use `dev-test` and `benchmark`: the ablation) **before** the first run on them (AGENTS.md 9.4.1), in the same change.
- **Q13. Estimate.** Default 1 day (raised from 0.5), with the plan confirmed by the maintainer; 0.5 day only with the scratch-script alternative of Q2.
- **Q14. A follow-up that is not in this task.** The metric summaries do not echo the cut-off of the layer that they read, so `(τ, d_min)` and the cut-off are not one echoed triple yet. A small task (X.NN, candidate: `cutoff_hz` and `filter_order` read from the layer's meta into `parameters` of `group_speed`, three lines and their tests) would make a pause number carry its filter. I **propose** it at the close and do not do it here (it touches P5.06 and P5.07 output).
- *Risk:* the conclusion is **dataset-specific**: video pose of an instructor, a Kinect of 12 performers and a white-noise generator are not the product's cameras; the finding is a hypothesis for the pilot, and the tool makes the re-run cheap.
- *Risk:* a real-data sensitivity does not say which cut-off is **right**: with no label of a pause there is no accuracy; the report must not read a lower paused share as a better one.
- *Risk:* D20 chunk-level numbers are dominated by the 45 % of rows that are not the target (`not_tracked`): the runs are short stretches; the report gives the number of measured rows beside every figure.
- *Risk:* the time of the full run (UMONS 150 s, D20 35 s, the generator about 2 minutes) is fine for a tool and too long for a test: the tests use the generator with few seeds and 2 UMONS files behind `dataset`.

## 2. Plan

**Design.**

1. `bench/filter_ablation.py` (pure, no I/O except through the arguments; no dataset content stored).
   - Constants: `CUTOFFS_HZ = (4.0, 6.0, 8.0)`, `NO_FILTER_HZ = 1000.0`, `REFERENCE_HZ = 6.0`, `PAUSE_GRID = ((0.1, 0.3), (0.2, 0.3), (0.3, 0.3), (0.5, 0.3), (0.2, 0.5))` (`τ` in Λ/s, `d_min` in s), `GROUPS = ("hands", "hips", "feet")`.
   - `layers_at_cutoffs(keypoints, scores, t_sec, *, tracked=None, in_segment=None, recording_id, frame_index, data_sources=(), cutoffs=(*CUTOFFS_HZ, NO_FILTER_HZ), base=None) -> dict[float, PoseFiltered]`: `run_quality_arrays` once per cut-off with `QualityParams` equal to `base` (default: the defaults) except `filter.cutoff_hz`, then `pose_filtered_from_result`; **checks the invariants** (same `t_grid`, `reason`, `interpolated`, `grid_in_segment` at every cut-off, else `AblationError` naming the cut-off and the array); a cut-off must be a finite number above 0, distinct, with `REFERENCE_HZ` among them. `layers_of_track(track, ...)` the same from a `TargetTrack` (`run_quality`).
   - `RunMeasure` (frozen): per group `n_measured`, `v_p50`, `v_p95` (Λ/s, over the measured rows), `pauses[(τ, d_min)]` (the tuple of `Pause`) and `series_len`, `measured` mask and the paused mask per cell (read-only arrays, kept for the agreement), `hip_range` of B (and A when a reference layer is given) or `None` with the reason name.
   - `measure_layer(layer, *, grid=PAUSE_GRID, reference=None) -> RunMeasure`: `group_speed` per group, `find_pauses` per cell on its series, `hip_height_b` with `stable_intervals` the whole segment (+ `hip_height_a` with a `reference`); a metric that has no evidence is recorded as `None` with its `insufficient_reason`, never as a zero and never swallowed.
   - `Agreement` (frozen) and `agree(measure, reference_measure, group, cell) -> Agreement`: `paused_share`, `intersection_rows`, `union_rows`, `count`, `count_equal`, `boundary_shifts` (matched pauses: each pause of one side is matched with the pause of the other with the largest overlap, at least half of the shorter one; the shifts of start and end in steps) and the numbers they come from, so that an aggregate can pool them.
   - `aggregate(runs: Sequence[Mapping[float, RunMeasure]]) -> AblationSummary`: per cut-off, group and cell: the sum of counts, the pooled paused share, the pooled IoU with 6 Hz, the share of runs with the same count, the distribution of the boundary shifts (median, p95, max), the V quantiles (median over runs), the hip range difference from 6 Hz (median, p95, max of the absolute relative difference), the number of runs and of measured rows.
   - `render_tables(summaries: Mapping[str, AblationSummary]) -> str` (markdown, deterministic, ids and numbers only) and `write_csv`.
   - Errors are `AblationError` (a `BenchError`) naming the argument.
2. `tools/filter_ablation.py`: `syn --out DIR [--seeds N]`, `d20 --pose DIR --out DIR`, `umons --umons ROOT --out DIR`, `all`; `--report FILE` writes the tables; exit codes as `umons_adapter.py` (0 all runs ran, 1 a run failed and is in the CSV as an error row, 2 the run could not start); `configure_logging`; only ids printed. `syn` builds the generator sweep of Q7 (the truth tables: floor, pause count and boundary error, hip range error), `d20` follows the target by `audit.analyse.target_track` and runs `run_quality` per cut-off, `umons` uses `load_kinect` and `run_quality_arrays` with its `tracked`.
3. Tests and the report as in the test plan; nothing is added to `src/` outside `bench/`; no change to `quality/`, `metrics/` or the schemas.
