## 1. Analysis

**Goal.** Variant B of Appendix B1, next to variant A in `metrics/hip_height.py`: `g(t) = max{ y'_j(t) : j in {15, 16, 24, 25}, joint has a value }` is the lowest point of the feet in the image (an estimate of the
floor), `h_B(t) = (g(t) - y'_H(t)) / Λ` with `Λ` the median trunk length of **the segment** (not of a reference), and the result is again `range = P95(h_B) - P5(h_B)` with the profile, the coverage, the reason of
every row without a value and the instants of the extremes. T-MET-04 checks it on SYN-02 (`range ≈ 2A`, 5 %); T-MET-05 checks **both variants against the Appendix B answer of the pinhole model** (SYN-09, six camera
geometries) and **records the numbers**. DEC-0014 already fixed the stance: both variants are computed and reported, **no order between them is asserted**, and the variant is chosen by the error against hand
measurements (G-MANUAL, P10.02, P10.06); a per-frame-Λ variant C is not added. No threshold is applied and the result is `experimental` (AGENTS.md 5.5).

**Three facts that shape the plan.**

1. **B is A with another numerator and another scale; everything else is the same.** The rows, the stable intervals and the flag (reasons 16 and 18), the pose reasons (1 to 6), the roll, the short-run rule (21), the series, the
   statistics and the events, the summary: all of it is A's tail. Copying it would make two files that drift; so the tail of `hip_height_a` is lifted into one private function that both variants call, and the 124
   tests of P5.03 are the safety net for A (Q7).
2. **B needs "the lowest of four", not "all of the joints".** `unusable_reason` (P5.02) gives the reason of the first joint of a list that has no value, which is the right rule for the two hips. For the feet the rule is
   "at least one of the four has a value; if none has, the reason of the first of the four in the order 15, 16, 24, 25" (Q1). Which joint is the lowest also matters for the flag of a bridged value: a row leans on a bridge when a
   hip is bridged **or the foot joint that was used is** (Q6). The lowest point is taken **after** the roll is taken out of every joint (Q3): the lowest point of the corrected image, not the rotated lowest point of the raw one.
3. **B has its scale in the segment and its sign is the opposite of A.** There is no reference layer, so no reason 20; `Λ` is the median of the trunk length over the valid rows of the segment (P5.02's `trunk_scale`), unavailable
   (19) when there is none or when those rows last less than an optional minimum (Q5). `h_B` is a true height (up is positive: `g - y_H`), where `h_A` is a vertical position (down is positive): so for B the event `max` is the
   **highest** hip. Both are in body lengths and both statistics are called `hip_height_range`; the metric name (`hip_height_a`, `hip_height_b`) tells them apart (DEC-0014).

**Conventions fixed by this task** (each is a place where P5.05 to P5.07 or the report could otherwise disagree):

1. **Same rows and same reasons as A, minus the reference.** A series row is a row of `grid_in_segment`; the order of the reasons is eligible (16, 18) → pose (hips, then feet: 1 to 6) → scale (19) → 0 → a measured run shorter
   than `min_valid_s` becomes 21. B never gives 20. A view is not a condition (17 is not used).
2. **`g`** is the maximum, over the joints 15, 16, 24, 25 that have a value in the row, of the vertical coordinate after `correct_roll` (origin `(0, 0)`, y down: the lowest point is the largest `y'`); ties go to the first joint
   of the list. A bridged foot value is a value. **`h_B = (g - y'_H) / Λ_segment`**, positive when the hip is above the lowest foot point; it does not depend on the origin or on a vertical shift of the image.
3. **`Λ_segment`** is the median of `L(t)` over **all valid rows of the segment** (finite and above 0), whether or not a row is eligible (Q2): Appendix B's definition and the truth of SYN-09.
4. **The reason of the feet** is 0 when at least one of the four has a value and otherwise the reason of the first of the four (15, 16, 24, 25); the hips come first in the order of the metric (11, 12, then the feet).
5. **The range, the events and the evidence rules are A's** (P95 - P5 with linear interpolation and at least two measured rows; events at the first row of equal values; no evidence gives no number: `no_frame`, `too_short`, or the
   most frequent reason). `bridged_rows` counts the measured rows with a bridged hip or a bridged foot joint that was used as `g`.
6. **No number is invented**: `min_scale_s` and `min_valid_s` are optional arguments (`None` = none); the summary echoes the parameters (`variant` `"b"`, `roll_deg`, `min_scale_s`, `min_valid_s`, the percentiles, `scale_px`,
   `scale_rows`, `scale_s`, `n_stable_intervals`); `experimental`, never conclusive.

**Constraints that apply (AGENTS.md section 5):** 1 no fabricated observation (a row without both hips or without any foot has no value; no range from fewer than two rows); 2 real timestamps (the grid of the layer);
3 layers stay apart; 4 units and coordinates documented, version, units, intervals, coverage and reasons carried; 5 no threshold; 7 no heavy work; 9 nothing personal; 11 no third-party code (the formula is ours, from Appendix B1).

**Unknowns, risks, questions** (answered with the defaults below so that the work can follow; each can be reversed before P5.05 uses it):

- **Q1. The reason when no foot joint has a value.** Default: **the reason of the first of 15, 16, 24, 25**, after the hips. Alternative: the most frequent of the four; or require both feet.
- **Q2. Which rows give `Λ_segment`.** Default: **all valid rows of the segment** (Appendix B; the truth of SYN-09). Alternative: only the eligible rows (inside the coach's intervals, unflagged), which is more careful and
  differs from the truth of SYN-09 only when part of a clip is excluded.
- **Q3. The roll and the lowest point.** Default: **the roll is taken out of every foot joint, then the largest `y'` is taken**. Alternative: take the lowest in the raw image and rotate that point (wrong when the lowest
  joint changes under the rotation).
- **Q4. Sign and events.** Default: B is a true height (up positive), so `max` is the highest hip, and the statistic keeps the name `hip_height_range`. Alternative: flip B's sign to look like A (it would then not be Appendix B's `g - y_H`).
- **Q5. A minimum for the segment scale.** Default: an optional `min_scale_s` (`None` = none), symmetric to A's `min_reference_s`, echoed in the summary. Alternative: no minimum at all for B.
- **Q6. `bridged_rows` for B.** Default: a measured row counts when a hip is bridged **or the foot joint that gave `g`** is. Alternative: any bridged joint of the four (counts a bridge that was not used).
- **Q7. The shared tail.** Default: **lift the tail of `hip_height_a` into a private function used by both variants** (A's behaviour unchanged; the 124 tests of P5.03 are the net). Alternative: copy it into `hip_height_b`
  (two files that drift) or put B in its own module (the plan row says the same module).
- **Q8. T-MET-05.** Default: six geometries (camera 0.6, 1.0, 1.3 m x depth x1.5, x2), noise 0 and 1 px, `flags = {}` because the walk of SYN-09 is a perspective shift by construction (on real data the flag would make every
  row 18 for both variants), each variant within `range_abs_error_lambda` (0.02 Λ) of **its own** truth, no order between A and B asserted (DEC-0014); the numbers are recorded in section 5 and in the DEC.
- **Q9. The set of foot joints.** Default: the four `GROUND_CANDIDATES` (ankles and heels), no toes (Appendix B1 and the generator's truth).
- **Q10. Estimate.** Default 1 day (raised from 0.5) with the shared tail; or 0.5 to 0.75 day if the refactor of A is left for later (Q7 alternative), which I do not recommend.
- *Risk:* on a depth change B is far from steady by construction (range 0.66 to 1.15 Λ against 0.06 to 0.37 for A on SYN-09): that is the known property, recorded not hidden; the flag `perspective_shift` is the guard for both.
- *Risk:* a foot that is off the floor (a kick, standing on one leg: two of the 24 forms) makes `g` not the floor and B wrong for that stretch; only the coach's stable intervals protect against it (as for A).
- *Risk:* the max of noisy feet is biased upwards by a fraction of a pixel; the bias is constant and cancels in a range, but it shifts the level (which is not reported).
- *Risk:* the tolerance of T-MET-05 is 0.02 Λ on ranges up to 1.15 Λ (under 2 %): the prototype leaves a factor 10; if the real code misses it, it is reported and asked, never loosened silently.

## 2. Plan

**Design.**

1. `hip_height.py`, one module with the two variants.
   - `_scale_of(rows: SegmentRows, minimum: float | None) -> ReferenceScale`: `trunk_scale(trunk_length(rows.keypoints))`, the duration `n_rows x step` (0 without a step), `NaN` when the minimum is not reached. `reference_scale`
     becomes `_scale_of(segment_rows(reference), minimum)`; B calls `_scale_of(rows, min_scale_s)` on its own segment. `ReferenceScale` keeps its name (A's; B uses the same object for its segment).
   - `_finish(rows, reason, values, *, name, variant, parameters, leaning, minimum_run, camera_id, view_category) -> HipHeightResult`: the part of `hip_height_a` after the reasons and values are known: `drop_short_runs`,
     the values of demoted rows set to NaN, `build_series` (with the metric name and `parameters`), `_statistics` (which now takes the per-row boolean `leaning` that each variant computes), `make_summary(..., "experimental")`.
   - `hip_height_a` computes its reasons and values as today, passes `leaning = bridged(kp, interp, HIP_JOINTS)`, and calls `_finish`. Its output must be identical to today's (same tests).
   - `hip_height_b` as the design in 2 to 4 below.
2. **Rows and scale.** `rows = segment_rows(layer)`; `eligible = eligibility(..., blocking=BLOCKING_FLAGS, name="stable_intervals")`; `scale = _scale_of(rows, min_scale_s)`; the "after" reason is 19 for every row when `scale` is NaN, else 0 (never 20).
3. **Pose reasons.** `hips = unusable_reason(kp, reason, HIP_JOINTS)`; `feet = _feet_reason(kp, reason)` = for the four joints in order, `unusable_reason` of each, then 0 where any is 0 and else the first column; the reason is
   `where(hips != 0, hips, feet)` (the hips come first in the order of the metric); then `where(eligible != 0, eligible, where(pose != 0, pose, after))`.
4. **Values.** Of the measured rows: `y = correct_roll(kp[:, [11, 12] + feet, :], roll)` split into the hip pair and the feet; `y_hip` is the mean of the two corrected hip `y'`; `y_feet` `(N, 4)` with NaN where a joint has no value;
   the selected column is the argmax of `where(valid, y_feet, -inf)` (the first on a tie); `g` is that value; `values = (g - y_hip) / scale`. The corrected `y'` of a mean equals the mean of the corrected `y'` (the rotation is
   linear), so the hip uses `mid_hip` and `correct_roll` as A does. `leaning = bridged(hips) | interpolated[row, selected foot joint]`.
5. Errors are `MetricError` naming the argument, as A's: layer not a `PoseFiltered`, bad intervals (`stable_intervals`), a flag not in the ontology or not a `bool`, a roll beyond 45 degrees or not a number (also when no row is
   measured), a minimum that is not `None` or a finite number above 0, a `camera_id` or `view_category` the header refuses. B has no `reference` argument.

- **Edge cases:** an empty segment (`no_frame`), a one-row segment (21), one and two measured rows, no stable interval, a flag, no valid trunk or too short a run of them (19), a hip missing, all four feet missing with four different reasons, one foot joint left, hips and feet missing together, a tie for the floor, a bridged hip and a bridged foot joint (used and not used), a roll that changes the lowest joint, a roll beyond 45 degrees or NaN (also with no measured row), a layer that is not a layer, a `reference` argument (a `TypeError`), a variable frame rate. - **Errors raised, never swallowed:** no `except`; every refusal is a `MetricError` naming its argument and its reason (read).
- **Tests fail when the behaviour breaks:** 40 of 40 mutants caught. The mutation check found one weak test (the tie rule) and one redundant line (the early roll check); both were fixed, neither changed a number.
- **No secrets, personal data or absolute paths** in the code, tests or docs of this task.
- **Docs:** W7 note, plan rows and sub-IDs, README, DEC-0057, CHANGELOG, TASK_STATUS.

**Left as it is, on purpose:** (1) a foot that is off the floor (a kick, standing on one leg) makes `g` not the floor and B wrong for that stretch; only the coach's stable intervals protect against it, as for A; (2) on a depth change B is far from steady by construction (it follows Λ(t) / Λ_median); the flag `perspective_shift` is the guard for both variants and the choice between A and B is made against hand measurements (P10.02, P10.06), not here; (3) the maximum of four noisy foot joints is biased upwards by a fraction of a pixel; the bias is constant and cancels in a range, and it is why the profile of B is a little noisier than A's (median error 0.002 to 0.004 Λ against 0.0013 to 0.0017); (4) the dataclass that carries a scale is still called `ReferenceScale`, now also for B's own segment; it keeps the public name that P5.03 and its tests use, and the docstring says so; (5) the events are the extremes of the filtered series (as for A).
