## 1. Analysis

**Goal.** The third MVP metric, Appendix B3: the speed of a group of joints on the **real clock**, in body lengths per second. For a joint `j` at a row `k`,
`v_j(t_k) = ‖p_j(t_{k+1}) - p_j(t_{k-1})‖ / (t_{k+1} - t_{k-1}) / Λ`; the speed of a group `G` (hands {9, 10}, hips {11, 12}, feet {15, 16, 24, 25}) is `V_G(t) = median over the joints of G that are usable of v_j(t)`. The result is the
**profile** `V_G` (the series), the original duration of the segment, the coefficient of variation of `V_G`, the coverage and the reason of every row without a value. The pauses (`V_G < τ` for at least `d_min`) are P5.07 and the
cut-off ablation is P5.08: both read this profile and add their own numbers, and neither is done here. No threshold is applied and the result is `experimental` (AGENTS.md 5.5).

**Source rules read.** Plan row P5.06 and test T-MET-08 (SYN-07: the speed on the real clock of a variable-frame-rate video, golden); the SYN-07 row of the tolerances (median relative speed error within 5 % on the valid frames,
"central difference on an irregular dt with the 6 Hz filter"; the generator's SYN-07 defaults: 4 s, 30 fps then 10 fps from 2 s, one pause from 1.0 to 1.5 s, **noise 0**); `Docs/TECH_WORKFLOW_V1.md` Appendix B (the

group, the original duration kept, the threshold and the minimum pause learned from the coach's labels for the same kind of movement; "the rhythm is judged on the original time axis"); `Docs/tasks/P0.07.md` (the generator:

**Four facts that shape the plan.**

1. **The neighbours of a row are rows of the layer, not of the series.** The series holds the rows of the segment, but the central difference of the first and of the last row needs the row before and the row after: they are in the
   margins that the pose pass read on purpose (W4: one second on each side). So the metric reads the segment's rows **and their two neighbours** from the layer's grid, and `run_edge` (22) is only for a row that has no neighbour
   in the grid at all (a segment at the very start or end of the track). A small shared function gives the neighbours (Q2).
2. **A joint is usable at a row when both neighbour cells have a value.** The formula never reads `p_j(t_k)` itself, so a single-frame hole at `k` costs nothing and a hole at `k - 1` or `k + 1` costs the joint. The group speed is the
   median of the usable joints; with none usable the row has the reason of the first joint of the group (its first missing neighbour). A bridged neighbour has a value and is counted and reported (P5.02 Q1) (Q3, Q4).
3. **The speed has a noise floor that is not small.** The central difference of two noisy positions has a standard deviation of about `σ √2 / (2 Δt) / Λ`; on the 6 Hz-filtered layer, 1 px of noise on a 200 px trunk leaves a floor of
   about 0.07 Λ/s on a joint that does not move, which is as large as the true speed of the hips in the generator's `raise_lower`. The tolerance of the plan (5 %) is a **noise-free** tolerance (the SYN-07 default has noise 0); the noisy
   numbers are measured, recorded and not hidden, and they tell P5.07 that a pause threshold has to sit above the floor of the footage (Q12).
4. **The truth has to exist at the times of the grid.** A variable-frame-rate video is resampled to a uniform grid whose times are not the frames, and the generator's speed truth is given at the frame times. The motion is analytic,
   so a public `speed_at(seq, t)` next to `truth_at` gives the truth at any time (the helper is test code; it gets its own independent test against a numerical derivative) (Q11).

**Out of scope.** The pauses and their count and lengths (P5.07, with `τ` and `d_min` from a config or a record); the ablation of the cut-off at 4, 6 and 8 Hz (P5.08: it needs the quality chain run with other cut-offs; the speed here is
computed on the final layer as it is, already low-passed); a roll correction (a speed is the norm of a displacement, which a rotation of the image does not change: no `roll_deg`); any threshold, minimum pause or "slow" label; a
one-sided difference at the edge of the grid (it would be another quantity); speeds in metres or in degrees; the comparison of rhythms between recordings (W8).

**Conventions fixed by this task** (each is a place where P5.07, P5.08 and the report could otherwise disagree):

1. **The value** `V_G(t_k)` is the median over the usable joints of the group of `‖p_j(k+1) - p_j(k-1)‖ / (t_{k+1} - t_{k-1}) / Λ`, in body lengths per second, with **the times of the grid** (`t_grid`, never an assumed step) and `Λ`
   the median trunk length of the valid rows of the segment (the scale of the segment, fixed for the whole series: v3 6.3's "fixed scale"; optional `min_scale_s`). The neighbours are the layer's grid rows, margins included.
2. **A joint is usable at a row** when the cells of the row before and of the row after have a value (observed or bridged); its own cell at the row is not read. The group needs one usable joint.
3. **Reasons of a row, in this precedence** (the first that applies): not eligible (16 outside the coach's intervals when given, 18 for the flag `perspective_shift`); then the pose, the reason (1 to 6) of the first group joint, in the
   order of the group, that is not usable, when no joint is (its first missing neighbour: the row before, then the row after); then 19 when the scale is unavailable; then 22 when the row has no neighbour on a side of the grid; then 0,
   measured; then a measured run shorter than `min_valid_s` becomes 21. A view is not a condition (17 is not used) and there is no reference (20).
4. **The statistics** are `segment_duration_s` (rows x the step of the grid, on the real clock), `speed_median`, `speed_cv` (the population standard deviation over the mean of the measured values, null when the mean is 0: never a
   NaN and never a 0 that nobody measured) and `bridged_rows` (measured rows that lean on a bridged neighbour of a usable joint). One measured row gives numbers; none gives `insufficient_evidence` with null statistics.
5. **No number is invented**: `min_valid_s` and `min_scale_s` are arguments (`None` = none); the summary echoes the parameters (`group`, `min_valid_s`, `min_scale_s`, `scale_px`, `scale_rows`, `scale_s`, `n_intervals`); `experimental`, never
   conclusive.

**Constraints that apply (AGENTS.md section 5):** 1 no fabricated observation (a row without a usable joint, without a neighbour or without a scale has a reason and no value; no one-sided difference, no fill); 2 real timestamps
(the difference is divided by the real `t_{k+1} - t_{k-1}`); 3 layers stay apart (the metric reads the final layer); 4 units (body lengths per second) and coordinates documented; 5 no threshold; 7 no heavy work; 9 nothing personal; 11 no
third-party code.

**Unknowns, risks, questions** (answered with the defaults below so that the work can follow; each can be reversed before P5.07 uses it):

- **Q1. One metric or three.** Default: **three metrics, `speed_hands`, `speed_hips`, `speed_feet`**, one function with `group`: the smallest useful change, the threshold and the minimum pause of the signed record differ by group (and
  P5.07's `pause_*` will follow). Alternative: one `speed` with the group in the parameters.
- **Q2. Where the neighbours come from.** Default: **the layer's grid, margins included**; `run_edge` (22) only where the grid has no row on one side. Alternative: only the rows of the segment, so that its first and last rows are always 22
  (it would waste the margins that W4 reads for this).
- **Q3. When a joint counts.** Default: **both neighbour cells have a value** (the own cell is not used by the formula); a bridged neighbour counts and is reported. Alternative: also require the own cell (it would drop a row for a hole that
  the formula does not read).
- **Q4. How many joints.** Default: **one usable joint is enough** for the median of a group; no minimum is invented (a record's `min_visible` will carry it). Risk: the feet group has four joints and may be left with one, which is noisier.
- **Q5. The scale.** Default: **the median trunk of the valid rows of the segment** (Appendix B), optional `min_scale_s` as for hip height B; the shared `scale_of` is lifted into `rows.py` and hip height B moves onto it with no change of
  output. Alternative: the scale of a reference standing segment as variant A (B3 says `Λ`, the scale of the segment, and v3 says a fixed scale: both are met by one value for the whole segment).
- **Q6. Flags, intervals, view.** Default: **`perspective_shift` blocks** (a depth change distorts a speed in trunk lengths, as B1 says of the height; the coach decides whether a walk in depth is flagged); the coach's intervals are
  **optional** (`None` = the whole segment: B3 keeps the original duration of the segment); the view is not a condition (B3 names none). Alternative: no blocking flag, which would give a number that a depth change distorts.
- **Q7. The statistics.** Default: `segment_duration_s`, `speed_median`, `speed_cv`, `bridged_rows`; no event; no pause. Alternative: also the P95 of `V_G` or the maximum (a number nobody asked for).
- **Q8. Evidence.** Default: **one measured row** gives a number. Alternative: two (a coefficient of variation of one value is 0, not a spread: with one row it is reported as 0.0 only if the mean is not 0, which is the arithmetic and
  not a claim; two rows would avoid the question).
- **Q9. The filter.** Default: **no filtering here**: the speed is read on the final layer as it is (already low-passed by the quality chain, 6 Hz by default); the ablation at 4, 6 and 8 Hz is P5.08 and needs layers made with those cut-offs.
- **Q10. The roll.** Default: **none**: a speed is the norm of a displacement and a rotation of the image does not change it.
- **Q11. The truth at grid times.** Default: add the public `speed_at(seq, t)` to the generator (test code) with an independent test against a 3000 fps numerical derivative of `truth_at`. Alternative: call the private `_motion` from the
  tests (a test would depend on a private name).
- **Q12. What T-MET-08 asserts.** Default: the plan's tolerance (**5 % on the median relative error**) at the **noise-free default of SYN-07** (and of SYN-05), on the **valid frames = measured rows whose true speed is at least 10 % of the
  peak of the group** (a relative error is undefined at zero speed; the 10 % is a test definition, not a product threshold); at noise 1 px the numbers are **measured and recorded** (the floor of a static group, the error of the fast
  group) and bounded only where they hold (the floor of a still group under 0.15 Λ/s; the hands within 15 %), with the hips' large error stated as a limit and not asserted.
- **Q13. Estimate.** Default 1 day (raised from 0.5); or 0.5 to 0.75 day if the generator helper and the lift of `scale_of` are left out (not recommended: the truth at grid times is what makes T-MET-08 meaningful on a resampled grid).
- *Risk:* the noise floor (about 0.07 Λ/s per joint at 1 px of noise) makes the speed of a slow group unreadable on noisy footage; P5.07 and P5.08 and the signed record have to set thresholds above the floor of the real footage, and the
  pilot (P9, P10) is what measures that floor. This task records the generator's floor so that the comparison exists.
- *Risk:* a performer who moves in depth changes the apparent size; the flag is the coach's guard and nothing in the pose checks it.
- *Risk:* the group median of two joints (hands, hips) is the mean of the two; of four (feet) the mean of the middle two; a group with one usable joint is that joint's speed.

## 2. Plan

**Design.**

1. `rows.py` (additive).
   - `Neighbours` (frozen dataclass) and `neighbour_rows(layer) -> Neighbours`: for the `N` rows of the segment (the rows of `grid_in_segment`), arrays of the grid row before and the grid row after: `keypoints_prev`, `keypoints_next`
     `(N, 26, 2)` float32, `reason_prev`, `reason_next` `(N, 26)` uint8, `interpolated_prev`, `interpolated_next` `(N, 26)` bool, `t_prev`, `t_next` `(N,)` float64, `has_prev`, `has_next` `(N,)` bool. Where the grid has no such row:
     positions NaN with the reason `no_frame` (6), flag False, time NaN, `has_*` False. Read-only copies; the same contiguity rule as `segment_rows`.
   - `ScaleEstimate(scale_px, n_rows, duration_s)` and `scale_of(rows, minimum)`: the median trunk of the valid rows of a `SegmentRows` (what `hip_height._scale_of` does); `hip_height.py` imports it and keeps `ReferenceScale = ScaleEstimate`.
2. `tempo.py`: `GROUP_METRIC_NAMES = {"hands": "speed_hands", "hips": "speed_hips", "feet": "speed_feet"}` (the joints are `pose.skeleton.GROUPS`), `BLOCKING_FLAGS = ("perspective_shift",)`.
   `group_speed`: validate `group`, the minimums; `rows = segment_rows(layer)`; `nb = neighbour_rows(layer)`; `eligible = eligibility(rows.t_sec, intervals=intervals, flags=flags, blocking=BLOCKING_FLAGS, name="intervals")`;
   `scale = scale_of(rows, min_scale_s)`; for each joint `j` of the group in order: `reason_prev_j = unusable_reason(nb.keypoints_prev, nb.reason_prev, [j])`, `reason_next_j` likewise, `usable_j = both 0`, `speed_j = hypot(kp_next[:, j] - kp_prev[:, j])
   / (t_next - t_prev) / scale` on the usable rows (NaN elsewhere), the reason of the joint the first non-zero of the two; `V = median of the usable joints` per row (a row loop-free `nanmedian` with the all-NaN case handled, no warning); the pose reason of a
   row with no usable joint is that of the first joint of the group; the edge rows (`~has_prev | ~has_next`) take no pose reason (nothing can be read); reasons `where(eligible, eligible, where(pose, pose, where(scale NaN, 19, where(edge, 22, 0))))`;
   `assemble_series`; the statistics from the measured rows; `make_summary(..., record_status="experimental")`.
3. `tests/helpers/synth.py`: `speed_at(seq, t)` returning `{group: (len(t),) float64}` in Λ/s from the analytic motion at the times `t` (inside `[0, duration]`), next to `truth_at`.
4. Errors are `MetricError` naming the argument: not a `PoseFiltered`, a `group` that is not `hands`, `hips` or `feet`, bad intervals, a flag that is not an ontology flag or not a `bool`, a minimum that is not `None` or a finite number above 0, a
   `camera_id` or `view_category` the header refuses.
