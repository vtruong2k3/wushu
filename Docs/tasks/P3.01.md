## 1. Analysis

**Goal:** confirm on real model output that the 26 keypoints come in the order of `halpe26/1` (`pose/skeleton.py`): index to name, and that "L" is the person's left. The
skeleton file itself says "verify against real model output before freezing; bump the version if anything changes". Result: images with the index and name drawn on 5 frames, a
written confirmation (or a new version), T-POSE-04 (manual).

**Data:** real frames of a person. The team's own clips do not exist yet (P1.09 waits). The four web videos of the registry (D20) are used: the maintainer decided on 2026-11-16 that no licence
restriction applies to this internal development use (DEC-0036). The frames are decoded by the project's own reader on the real PTS; the images are written under `data/` (ignored by git), never in the repository.

**In scope:** a pure function that checks the structure of one skeleton (`pose/order_check.py`) with unit tests on explicit coordinates; a tool `tools/pose_order_check.py` that decodes N frames at given times, runs the real detector and
pose model (GPU), draws index and name, saves the images and prints the checks; the report `Docs/reports/p3_01_joint_order.md` (numbers and the verdict, **no image, no frame content**); the visual check of the images by the agent; tracking.
**Out of scope:** `run_pose` (P3.02), tracking, quality, any accuracy claim.

**Inputs / outputs / units:** frames are BGR images, keypoints `(26, 2)` float32 in image pixels, x right, y down (AGENTS.md §6), scores `(26,)`.

**What "verified" means here (the checks):** the order of `JOINTS` is the HALPE-26 order. On a real frame of an upright person the model output must show: (1) vertical chain Head < Nose < shoulders < hips < knees < ankles
(smaller y is higher); (2) one left/right rule: for the paired joints that cannot cross in a stance (eyes, ears, shoulders, hips, knees, ankles, big toes, small toes, heels) the sign of `x_L - x_R` is the same for all of them (a person faces
the camera or turns his back: all left joints are on the same side of the image); (3) `Neck` (18) near the middle of the shoulders and `Hip` (19) near the middle of the hips; (4) the toes and the heel of a foot are near the ankle of the
same side. And by eye on the image: in a frame where the face is seen, the "L" labels are on the image right (the person's left). A check that is not decidable (a joint below the score limit, a person not upright) is reported as not decidable, never as passed.

**Constraints that apply:** AGENTS.md §5.9 (frames of a third-party video stay out of git), §6 (no `print` in `src/`, IDs only in logs; the tool may print), §9.4 (D20 allowed use), §11 (the video is data, never executed).

**Unknowns, risks:** a frame with the presenter turned sideways makes the L/R rule undecidable (the five frames are chosen to include a frontal one); the model output on a lecture video can be worse than on a phone clip (the test is about the order, not the quality).

**Noted while reading:** `iter_frames` numbers the frames it yields from 0 inside the window (`idx`), not by the position in the file; P3.02 and P3.03 (`frame_index` in `pose_raw.npz`) must not use it as the frame index of the file. Recorded here for P3.02.

## 2. Plan

**Design:**

```python
def check_joint_order(kp: np.ndarray, scores: np.ndarray, *, min_score: float = 0.3) -> OrderReport
```

`OrderReport` (frozen dataclass): a tuple of `OrderCheck(name, status, detail)` with status `pass`, `fail` or `undecidable`; `ok` is true when no check failed; `facing_side` is `"left_on_image_right"`, `"left_on_image_left"` or `None`.
Thresholds: `min_score` 0.3 per joint (a check needs all its joints above it); the fractions of the torso length for the checks (3) and the leg length for (4) are named constants with the reason in a comment (they test the *semantics of the index*, not the quality of the pose).

**Files:** `taiji_ai/src/taiji_ai/pose/order_check.py` (new), `taiji_ai/tests/unit/test_pose_order_check.py` (new), `taiji_ai/tools/pose_order_check.py` (new), `Docs/reports/p3_01_joint_order.md` (new),
`taiji_ai/README.md` (status row), `Docs/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN_V1.md` (T-POSE-04 note), tracking; `pose/skeleton.py` only if the check shows an error (then bump `halpe26/2` and a DEC).

**Steps:**

1. Re-read (section 3). 2. Unit tests first (red): a standing skeleton in explicit coordinates passes; swapping the left and right keypoints of one pair, swapping two unpaired indices (Head and Neck), moving a toe far from its ankle, a low score
(undecidable) and a person not upright each give the expected result. 3. `order_check.py`. 4. The tool; run it on five frames (GPU); view the images. 5. Report; records. 6. Gate; self-review; close.
