## 1. Analysis

**Goal:** check the audio offset with an independent visual signal. The capture protocol (§5) switches a light on with each clap,
visible to both cameras; the frame where the light first appears in A and in B, mapped through the audio sync, must agree. The
difference is the residual that decides whether the two cameras may be combined in metrics (P2.06).

**Facts read (2026-11-16):**
- W2 step 4 (validate by a visible marker, residual in ms and frames).
- T-SYNC-04 (flash at a known frame: found at that frame); protocol locks exposure (§4), so a light causes a step in the mean brightness.
- Data contract: `SyncValidation` (`schemas/sync.py`, `sync/1` schema) defines the contract:
  - `method: Literal["flash", "none"]`
  - `flash_a_s: tuple[Seconds, ...]` (flash onsets seen by camera A on its timeline)
  - `flash_b_s: tuple[Seconds, ...]` (flash onsets seen by camera B on its timeline)
  - `residual_start_ms: NonNeg | None` (|t_b - a_to_b(t_a)| at start flash [ms])
  - `residual_end_ms: NonNeg | None` (same at end flash [ms])
  - `residual_ms: NonNeg | None` (max(start, end) [ms])
  - `residual_frames: NonNeg | None` (residual_ms / 1000 / frame_dt_s)
  - `frame_dt_s: Positive | None` (median frame interval of the slower camera)
- `iter_frames` / PyAV decoding decodes frames with real PTS timestamps (t_sec = (pts - start) x time_base).
- Audio sync mapping: a_to_b(t_a) = t_a + offset_s + drift x (t_a - t_ref_s).

**In scope:**
1. `io/flash.py`:
   - `brightness_series(path, t0, t1, rotation_deg, step) -> (t_sec, luma)`: fast mean luma per frame on subsampled grid (`f.to_ndarray(format="gray")[::step, ::step]`), real timestamps.
   - `FlashParams(min_step=15.0, k_mad=5.0, min_separation_s=0.3, search_margin_s=2.0)`.
   - `find_flash_onsets(t_sec, luma, params) -> list[float]`: rising step detection using robust spread (MAD), onset timestamp is the first bright frame.
   - `validate_sync(audio_sync, rec_dir_a, rec_dir_b, params, settings) -> SyncValidation`: pairs onsets of A and B in start and end windows, calculates residual in ms and in frames of slower camera (max(dt_A, dt_B)).
2. Tests: `tests/contract/test_flash.py` covering T-SYNC-04, T-SYNC-04b..d.

**Out of scope:** the usability rule and writing `sync.json` or database row (P2.06), non-flash markers.

**Design & Math:**
- A rising step is detected where frame-to-frame difference Delta L[i] = L[i] - L[i-1] satisfies:
  Delta L[i] >= max(min_step, median(Delta L) + k_mad x 1.4826 x MAD(Delta L)).
- The onset timestamp is t_sec[i] (the first illuminated frame).
- Consecutive onsets must be separated by >= min_separation_s (refractory period to avoid multiple detections while a 0.5s flash remains on).
- If `audio_sync.status != "ok"`, sync mapping is unavailable -> returns `SyncValidation(method="none", ...)`.
- Onsets in Camera A within start/end windows are mapped to Camera B via a_to_b(t_a); matched with Camera B onsets in the corresponding time interval.
- Residual is |t_b - a_to_b(t_a)|. Start and end residuals are combined by max(start, end).
