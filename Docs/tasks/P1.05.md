## 1. Analysis

**Goal:** a protocol that lets any group record a 24-form take with two phones so that the video can be ingested with
true timestamps (P2.02), synchronised (P2.04) and measured (P5). Bad footage cannot be repaired later, so the protocol
is the cheapest quality control of the whole project.

**Source of the requirements:** `TECH_WORKFLOW_V1.md` §3 W0 (the plan row lists: position on the form axis, a frame-rate
lock app, clap and flash at the start and end, a standing reference, a checkerboard) and decision 7 of §12 (checkerboard
in the first session if it adds under 5 minutes).

**In scope (content of `Docs/capture_protocol.md`):**

1. Roles and equipment checklist (two phones, two tripods, a light for the flash cue, a printed checkerboard).
2. Camera placement: one camera on the main direction of the form, one at 90°; height, distance and framing rules; the
   diagonal parts of the form are recorded as expected low coverage (W0). Each camera is level (roll near 0, using the
   phone's level or grid), and if possible a true vertical line stays in view (a door frame, a plumb line): trunk lean
   (Appendix B2) is corrected by a camera roll measured on such a line.
3. Camera settings: 1080p, fixed frame rate (30 fps by default), locked exposure, focus and zoom; an app that locks the
   frame rate (the examples in W0); stabilisation, HDR and beauty filters off; landscape with orientation locked;
   microphone on (audio is the sync signal).
4. Sync cues: two claps with a visible flash at the start and at the end of every take; nobody speaks near the phones.
5. Reference: 3–5 s of the performer standing upright at the start position (scale and stand angle).
6. Checkerboard: a short scan per session (specification in Q4).
7. Per take metadata (W0): `session_id`, `participant_id` (pseudonym), `take_id`, `camera_id`, camera position and
   height, form direction, `consent_ref`; file naming without names (for example `S01-P03-T02-camA`).
8. Handling: copy the original files by cable or card, never through a messaging app (re-encoding, lost audio or
   rotation); never edit or trim the originals.
9. Session time budget (W0: 1.5–2 hours per group session) and a before / during / after checklist.

**Out of scope:** doing the trial (P1.09), choosing the final distances (measured in the trial and written back as
version 1.1 of the protocol), ingest code (P2).

**Constraints that apply:** AGENTS.md §5.2 (real timestamps: the protocol must prevent VFR and record rotation), §5.9
and §9.5 (a recording enters the system only with a pseudonym and a consent reference; faces are personal data, so no
posting or sending to services), TECH_WORKFLOW W0.

**Unknown today:** which phones and apps will be used (`Docs/hardware_inventory.md` lists no camera), the floor space
of the routine (decides the distance), the lighting of the venue.

**Questions for the maintainer:**

| #  | Question | Default if no answer |
| -- | -------- | -------------------- |
| Q1 | Which two phones and which recording app? | The protocol lists the required settings, not a model; the trial records what the phones can do |
| Q2 | Frame rate: 30 or 60 fps | 30 fps: the Hampel window of P4.03 is counted in samples and P4.08 is tested on 30 Hz data, so 30 fps keeps those settings comparable, and files are half the size; 60 fps only if the trial shows motion blur on the arms |
| Q3 | Codec | H.264 if the phone offers it; HEVC is accepted if `probe_video` and `scan_pts` read it |
| Q4 | Checkerboard | A large rigid board, about 9 by 6 inner corners with squares of roughly 80–100 mm (about 1 m by 0.7 m): at 4–5 m a phone at 1080p shows roughly 3–4 mm per pixel, so a 25 mm square would cover only about 7 pixels (estimate, checked in the trial). One scan of about 1 minute per session, moving the board through the capture area (decision 7) |
| Q5 | Venue and date of the trial | needed for P1.09 |

- **Findings that change the text of the protocol (the plan's steps stay):**
  1. The naming rule must follow the database: `take_id` is **the same for both cameras of one take** (the `sync` key is
     `take_id` + two camera ids) and `camera_id` tells them apart. So `take_id = <session>-<participant>-<take>` (for example
     `S01-P03-T02`), `camera_id` is `camA` or `camB`, and a file is `<take_id>-<camera_id>.<ext>`. This is the plan's example
     read as two fields.
  2. Q1–Q4 have no answer from the maintainer, so the plan defaults are used (no phone model, 30 fps, H.264 preferred and
     HEVC accepted if `probe_video` and `scan_pts` read it, the 9 by 6 board). Q5 (venue and date) stays open and blocks only
     P1.09.
  3. The protocol is read by the people who film, so it is written in Vietnamese like the other field-facing files; the
     distances and heights are **starting values marked "(thử)"** that P1.09 replaces in version 1.1 (AC2).
  4. The dry-run of step 3 can only be done by the agent reading as a filming person now; a dry-run by O or H is part of
     P1.09. AC1 (C-4) and AC2 cannot be met before P1.09, so P1.05 stays `IN_REVIEW` after version 1.0 is written.
