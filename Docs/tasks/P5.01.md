## 1. Analysis

**Goal.** Every metric of W7 (hip height A and B, trunk lean, tempo and pause; P5.03 to P5.07) must return its result in one shape that says,
next to the number, what it is in (units, version), where it holds (valid intervals), how much of the performance it covers and why the rest
is missing (`missing_reason`). P5.01 defines that shape and the rules that make it honest: a metric with no evidence returns
`insufficient_evidence` and **no number, never 0** (plan, acceptance of P5), a metric without a signed `metric_record` is `experimental` and
concludes nothing (AGENTS.md 5.5), and the three coverage ratios of v3 section 8.3 can be computed from what is stored (P10.04 divides, P8 shows).
No metric is computed here.

**Source rules read.** Plan row P5.01 and T-SCH-02 ("`MetricSeries` requires units, valid_intervals, coverage"); W7 and Appendix B of
`Docs/TECH_WORKFLOW_V1.md` (all coordinates are pixels, y down; `metric_version`, `units`, `valid_intervals`, `coverage`, `missing_reason` per metric;
no common threshold); section 8.2 and 8.3 there and v3 section 8.2/8.3 (the record fields; the three ratios per metric and camera, "coverage with a
denominator"); the table `metric_record` (migration 0001: `status` is `experimental`, `enabled` or `retired`; `version` is text); DEC-0014 (A and B are two
metrics, no ordering asserted); T-MET-07, T-MET-11, P5.09, P8.10, P10.04, P10.06 (who will read these types); the style of `quality.json` (DEC-0050:
counts with their denominators, no fractions, partitions checked by validators) and of the layer files (DEC-0053: the reason array as the single source
of truth, checks of a file against itself). Existing code: `schemas/_base.py`, `schemas/quality.py`, `schemas/quality_layers.py`, `quality/mask.py`
(`REASON_NAMES`), `quality/layers_io.py` (`PoseFiltered`: the layer that P5.02 to P5.07 will read), `metrics/__init__.py` (empty).

**In scope.**

- `schemas/metric.py` (Pydantic v2, `extra="forbid"`, frozen; `schema_version` on the file model): `Unit`, the vocabulary `MissingReason`, `Interval`, `Coverage`,
  `MissingCounts`, `MetricHeader`, `Statistic`, `MetricEvent`, `MetricSummary` (the entry of `metric_summary.json`) and `MetricSummaryFile` (the file).
- `metrics/series.py`: `MetricSeries` (a header and three arrays: `t_sec`, `values`, `reason`), `build_series` (derives intervals, coverage and counts from the
  reason array) and `check_series`; `make_summary` (a summary from a series, so that no metric re-derives the header).
- Tests T-SCH-02a..g, the doc notes, DEC-0054, tracking.

**Out of scope.** Any computation of a metric (P5.02 to P5.07); `metric_record` rows and their loader (P5.09: this task only has the status field they will
fill); the files of a run, their names and paths (`metrics.parquet`, `metric_summary.json`: P7; `MetricSummaryFile` is only the model that P7 will write with
`write_manifest`); thresholds and any "good or bad" (AGENTS.md 5.5); the three ratios per metric and camera over many segments (P10.04 sums the counts of
this task); the view ontology and the segment flags (the caller passes the reason of a row; this task does not read the segment table).

**The shape (decided from the sources above).**

*One row of a series = one grid row of the final layer inside the segment* (`pose_filtered.npz`, `grid_in_segment`), at its real time `t_sec` (float64,
seconds from the stream start, strictly increasing, uniform step `step_s`). A series has `values` (float64) and `reason` (uint8, one per row): **`reason` 0
means measured and `values` is finite; any other reason means `values` is NaN and the reason says why** — the same contract as the mask layer, and the
reason is the single source of truth: everything else in the header is derived from it and the reader recomputes it.

*The reason codes* (`MissingReason`, closed): **0 measured; 1 to 6 are the six reasons of the quality layer with the same numbers** (`low_conf`,
`non_finite`, `not_tracked`, `frame_invalid`, `outlier`, `no_frame`: copied through from the first unusable required joint, so nothing is mapped); 7 to 15 are reserved;
**16 `outside_interval`** (the row is not in an interval the coach chose for this metric), **17 `view_not_supported`** (the view of the interval is not one the metric
supports), **18 `interval_flagged`** (an interval flag such as `perspective_shift` makes the metric invalid), **19 `scale_unavailable`** (the scale Λ cannot be
measured), **20 `reference_missing`** (hip variant A has no standing reference segment), **21 `too_short`** (a valid run shorter than the minimum duration),
**22 `run_edge`** (the central difference of the speed has no neighbour on one side). Adding a reason bumps the version of the schema.

*The three coverage numbers of v3 8.3 are counts of rows with one nesting*, in this order of precedence for a row: 16 to 18 mean *not eligible*; then 1 to 6
mean *eligible but the pose is not good enough*; then 19 to 22 mean *good pose but not measured*; then 0 measured. So with `total` rows,
`eligible = total - (rows with 16..18)`, `quality = eligible - (rows with 1..6)`, `measured = rows with 0`, and **`measured <= quality <= eligible <= total`**. Ratio 1 =
`eligible / total`, ratio 2 = `quality / eligible`, ratio 3 = `measured / total`, and `missing_by_reason` (one count per reason, fixed fields like the
`ReasonCounts` of `quality.json`) adds up to `total - measured`. The model stores the counts and `step_s` (seconds = rows x `step_s`, as `duration_s` in
`quality.json`); the ratios are methods that return **`None` when the denominator is 0**, never 0 and never NaN.

*Valid intervals* are the maximal runs of measured rows, each `[t_first, t_last + step_s)` in seconds (half open, like the named intervals of the coverage), ordered
and disjoint; the series check recomputes them from `reason`.

*Evidence and record status.* `MetricSummary.evidence` is `sufficient` or `insufficient_evidence`. **`insufficient_evidence` has no number**: every
statistic value is `null`, there is no event, and `insufficient_reason` (a `MissingReason` name) says why; `sufficient` has `insufficient_reason = null`, at least one
valid interval and at least one statistic with a value. `record_status` is the vocabulary of the table (`experimental`, `enabled`, `retired`); `enabled` needs
`record_version` (the version of the signed record), the others forbid it; `MetricSummary.conclusive` is true only for `sufficient` and `enabled` (T-MET-11: an
`experimental` metric shows its value and draws no conclusion). An output that depends on a threshold (a pause count, a duration over an angle) has
`uses_threshold = true`; the summary then says where the threshold came from, `threshold_source` = `signed_record` (needs `enabled`) or `unsigned_config` (for P5.07's
"τ and d_min from config" and the ablation of P5.08: allowed only while `experimental`), and `null` when no output uses one.

*Statistics and events.* `Statistic` = `name` (`^[a-z][a-z0-9_]{1,63}$`), `value` (finite float or `null`), `units`, `uses_threshold`; names are distinct. `MetricEvent` = `kind`
(`max`, `min`, `pause`), `t_start`, `t_end`, `value` (finite or `null`), `uses_threshold`; an extremum has `t_start == t_end` and no threshold, a pause has `t_start < t_end` and always `uses_threshold` (it is defined by τ and d_min); **every event lies inside a valid interval**
(the check refuses an event in a hole). `parameters` is a bounded map of what the metric used (`variant`, `group`, `cutoff_hz`, `lambda_px`, `roll_deg`, `tau`, ...): keys
`^[a-z][a-z0-9_]{0,63}$`, values a string `^[A-Za-z0-9_.-]{0,64}$`, an integer, a finite float, a bool or `null`; at most 32 entries; no path, no free text.

*Header.* `metric_name` (`^[a-z][a-z0-9_]{1,63}$`; hip A and B are `hip_height_a` and `hip_height_b`, DEC-0014), `metric_version` (text, a positive integer: the table
`metric_record` keys on name and version as text), `units`, `recording_id`, `camera_id`, `view_category` (`^[a-z_]{1,32}$`: the ontology owns the valid values, not this file),
`step_s` (positive or `null` for fewer than two rows), `valid_intervals`, `coverage`, `missing_by_reason`, `parameters`, `data_sources` (the same `DataSource` of AGENTS.md 9.4.6
as the layer files: an empty list means one of our own recordings). No path, file name or person's name; no wall clock. `MetricSummaryFile` (`schema_version`
`metric_summary/1`) holds the entries of one run: distinct (name, version), one `recording_id` and one `camera_id`.

**Unknowns, risks, questions** (answered with the defaults below because P5.02 to P5.07 cannot start without a shape; each can be changed before P5.03 uses it):

- **Q1. Two files or one?** Default: the Pydantic models in `schemas/metric.py` (as the plan row says) and the array dataclass `MetricSeries` in `metrics/series.py`, because
  `schemas/` holds JSON models and the arrays belong next to the metrics. The plan row names only `schemas/metric.py`; the row is amended with a dated note.
- **Q2. Counts or ratios?** Default: counts and `step_s`, ratios as methods (the rule of `quality.json`, DEC-0050; P10.04 sums counts across segments, which ratios cannot do).
- **Q3. A closed vocabulary of reasons**, numbered like the quality reasons for 1 to 6. Alternative: free strings (a typo becomes a new reason; the counts could not be a fixed
  table).
- **Q4. `record_status` with the table's three values and `conclusive` as a property.** Alternative: only `experimental` now and the rest in P5.09 (a schema bump later).
- **Q5. `threshold_source`.** P5.07 says that τ and d_min come from config and the ablation of P5.08 needs thresholds before any record exists; AGENTS.md 5.5 allows a
  threshold only from a signed record. Default: an unsigned threshold is allowed only as `unsigned_config`, labelled, and only in an `experimental` summary (so it can be shown
  as a value and never as a conclusion). Alternative: forbid any threshold output without a record and let P5.07 return the profile only.
- **Q6. A series of 0 rows** (a segment with no grid row) is allowed: it is `insufficient_evidence` with `total = 0` and ratios `None`; one row is allowed only unmeasured (no step, so no duration).
- *Risk:* a reason is a single label per row but a metric uses several joints; the rule "the reason of the first unusable required joint, in the order of the joints of
  the metric" is the business of P5.02 to P5.07, and each of them states it. The vocabulary here has room for it (1 to 6).
- *Risk:* the three ratios depend on a row being *eligible*, which needs the segment table (view, flags); this task only fixes how the result is counted. The caller of the
  metric (P7) passes the eligibility as the reasons 16 to 18.

## 2. Plan

**Design.**

1. `schemas/metric.py`: `Unit` (`Literal`: `body_lengths`, `body_lengths_per_s`, `deg`, `px`, `s`, `count`, `ratio`), `MissingReason` (an `IntEnum`: names and numbers above; the
   numbers 1 to 6 are asserted equal to `quality.mask.REASON_NAMES`), `ELIGIBILITY`, `QUALITY`, `AFTER_QUALITY` (the three groups of codes), `Interval` (`t_start < t_end`),
   `MissingCounts` (a field per reason, `NonNegInt`), `Coverage` (`total`, `eligible`, `quality`, `measured` rows; the nesting validator; `eligible_ratio()`,
   `quality_ratio()`, `measured_ratio()`), `MetricHeader`, `Statistic`, `MetricEvent`, `MetricSummary(MetricHeader)` and `MetricSummaryFile`. Validators: intervals ordered and
   disjoint; `MissingCounts` adds up to `total - measured`; the nesting of the counts; `Coverage` agrees with `MissingCounts` (`eligible = total - n(16..18)`, `quality = eligible - n(1..6)`,
   `measured = quality - n(19..22)`); evidence rules; `record_status` and `record_version`; `threshold_source`; events inside valid intervals; names distinct.
2. `metrics/series.py`: `MetricSeries(header, t_sec, values, reason)` (frozen dataclass, arrays read-only; `__post_init__` runs `check_series`, so an invalid series cannot exist);
   `build_series(*, metric_name, metric_version, units, recording_id, camera_id, view_category, t_sec, values, reason, step_s, parameters=None, data_sources=())` takes
   `step_s` from the grid (`PoseFiltered.meta.timeline.step_s`; an identity grid of a clock in whole milliseconds is uniform only to 1 ms, so the step is given and not recomputed from the
   times), derives the valid intervals from the runs of `reason == 0` (`[t_first, t_last + step_s)`), the coverage and the counts, and returns a `MetricSeries`; `check_series` is the
   one check (dtypes, shapes, `t_sec` finite and strictly increasing with a mean interval within 1 % of `step_s`, `values` finite exactly where `reason == 0` and NaN elsewhere, reason codes
   known, and the header equal to what the arrays give). Coverage in seconds is rows x `step_s` (the definition), not the length of the intervals. A series of fewer than two rows has no
   step (`step_s` null) and **no measured row**; `MetricError` (in `metrics/__init__.py`, like `QualityError`) is the exception of the metrics; `make_summary(series, *, statistics, events=(), record_status="experimental", record_version=None, threshold_source=None, insufficient_reason=None)` fills the header from the series and
   sets `evidence` (`insufficient_evidence` when `insufficient_reason` is given, else `sufficient`).
3. The reason array is copied, never filled: a NaN stays NaN with its reason; no value is invented to make a series look complete.
