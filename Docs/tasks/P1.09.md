## 1. Analysis

**Goal:** two real videos (one take, two cameras) recorded by the protocol, with metadata, that prove the protocol works
and serve as the first real data for P2 (ingest and sync) and P3 (pose). They are the "F-TRIAL" data of the plan
(`data/`, never committed).

**Agent role:** prepare the day (checklist, metadata sheet), check the files with the existing reading code
(`probe_video`, `scan_pts`, `is_vfr` from P0.03), write the result and the protocol corrections. The agent cannot
record, so people do the filming.

**In scope:**

1. Before: protocol version of P1.05, performer's consent (below), pseudonym, two phones, tripods, checkerboard.
2. The take: clap and flash cue at the start; 3–5 s standing reference; the whole 24-form; clap and flash at the end;
   a checkerboard scan; the metadata sheet filled in (W0 fields).
3. After: copy by cable or card into `taiji_ai/data/trial/` (git-ignored: `data/*` is ignored, `*.mp4` as well); record
   the SHA-256 of each file; run the checks below; list the defects.

**Checks on the files (read-only use of existing code):** per video resolution, nominal and average fps, `vfr` flag
(`is_vfr` on the PTS from `scan_pts`), rotation, audio present, duration; the two cameras have the same fps; the clap and
the flash are visible in both videos at the start and the end (manual look at the frames, and the audio peak); the
standing reference is at least 3 s; the performer is inside the frame during the whole form in both views; the
checkerboard corners are found by OpenCV (`findChessboardCorners`, `opencv-python-headless` is already a dependency) on
a few frames of the scan in both cameras. Optional: run `PoseEstimator` (`pose/rtm.py`, P0.02) on a few decoded frames
per camera from a scratch script outside the repository, to see that the performer is detected; the real run is P3.
(`tools/env_check.py --gpu-smoke` uses a synthetic frame, so it cannot show this.)

**Records to update:** the D08 row of `Docs/DATASET_REGISTRY_TAIJI.md` (first local recording: a trial take, its
allowed use as the consent says, no training unless `consent_train = 1`), and `Docs/hardware_inventory.md` (phone models,
OS and app versions; no personal data).

**Out of scope:** the ingest manifest and sync (P2), the real pose run (P3), more than one take.

**Constraints that apply:** AGENTS.md §9.5 (a recording enters the system only with a pseudonym and a consent
reference), §5.9 (videos never in git), §5.2 (the PTS are read, not assumed).

**Observations on the plan (not changed here; the maintainer decides):**
