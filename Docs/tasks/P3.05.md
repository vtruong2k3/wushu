## 1. Analysis

**Goal:** give every eligible person of every frame of a `PoseRaw` a **track id**, so that the performer chosen by P3.04 can be followed through the segment without ever jumping to another person (the plan's rule: "do not jump to another person", SYN-12:
zero identity swaps). This task does the *association* (who in frame `t` is who in frame `t - 1`); the *lost-target state* (how long a target may be missing, when it may be re-attached, `target_lost`, `track.parquet`) is P3.06.

**What the plan, workflow and code say (read 2026-11-16):**

- Plan row P3.05 and tests T-TRK-02 (SYN-12: two people walking close to each other, no ID swap) and T-TRK-03 (Hungarian with a changing number of people between frames). W5 step 2: match frame to frame with the Hungarian algorithm on a combined cost, `1 - IoU(bbox)` and the mean keypoint
  distance normalised by the bbox height; the plan names `sort_people_sports2d` (Pose2Sim, BSD-3-Clause, not cloned under `repos/`: only `REPOS.md` mentions it) as a reference and asks to keep its notice if code is copied. **No code is copied here**: the function is written from the W5 description; the
  idea source is recorded in the code and in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` only if the maintainer wants the reference read (Q9).
- SYN-12 (`tests/helpers/synth.py`): target (scale 1.0, x about 700 with a slight sway, absent from 1.5 to 2.5 s), distractor (0.95, walks left at 400 px/s through the target's place at `cross_s`), bystander (0.9, still at x = 1500); detections in a shuffled order per frame;
  `truth["person_id"]` (T, N) gives the identity of each slot; the tolerance table says `identity_swaps = 0`. `expected_target_lost` (P3.06) is not used here.
- `PoseRaw`: person rows in compressed form; `bboxes` (the detector's box on a detector frame, a box derived from the previous keypoints in between: **the box convention changes with the cadence N**, which P3.07 will tune), `keypoints`, `scores`, `t_sec` (real PTS, not a constant interval), `person_offsets`,
  `params.min_score` and `min_valid_keypoints`. `valid_keypoint_mask` (P3.04) is the shared rule for valid keypoints; `select_target` returns the person row of the performer.
- `scipy>=1.14` is a dependency (`scipy.optimize.linear_sum_assignment`).

**What the Hungarian step must resolve (worked out while reading, the reason for the first step of the plan).** In SYN-12 the distractor passes through the target's place. At the crossing the two people nearly coincide, so a cost that only compares each person with the *last position* of each track
is nearly symmetric: with the target still and the distractor moving by 13 px per frame the "straight" and the "swapped" assignments have about the same total cost; only the 5 % difference of scale and the motion separate them. A constant-velocity prediction of each track (last pose
plus velocity times the real time step) makes the straight assignment cost about 0 and the swapped one about 26 px. Step 2 of the plan measures this on SYN-12 (several seeds and crossing speeds) **before** the design is frozen: prediction on or off is decided by the numbers, not by this reading.

**In scope:** `track/associate.py`: `associate_tracks(raw, *, params=None) -> Tracks`, `AssociationParams`, `Tracks`, `track_of_person`; the cost; the gate; short gaps; tests (unit on explicit people, SYN-12, a real clip); README, plan rows, W5 text, tracking.
**Out of scope:** the lost-target state, `max_lost`, re-attachment by threshold and `track.parquet` (P3.06); smoothing or interpolation of keypoints (P4); appearance features; saving the tracks (P3.06 writes `track.parquet`).

**Inputs / outputs / units:** input `PoseRaw` (pixels, y down, seconds from the stream start). Output `Tracks` (frozen, arrays aligned with the person rows of the `PoseRaw`):

| Field | Shape / type | Meaning |
| ----- | ------------ | ------- |
| `match_margin` | (D,) float32 | how contested the match is: the smallest cost of any other live track for this person, or of any other person for this track, minus the chosen cost; **NaN** when there is no competitor; small or negative: the identity is uncertain here (a crossing) |
| `track_id` | (D,) int32 | track of each person row; ids start at 0 in order of first appearance; **-1** = not tracked (a weak person or an unusable box) |
| `match_cost` | (D,) float32 | the combined cost of the match to the previous person of the track; **NaN** when there is none (a track start, or not tracked): not 0 |
| `gap_frames` | (D,) int32 | frames since the previous person of the track (1 = the previous frame; 0 for a track start; -1 for not tracked) |
| `n_tracks` | int | number of tracks |
| `track_first_frame`, `track_last_frame`, `track_n_persons` | (K,) int32 | per track: frame positions of its first and last person and its number of persons |
| `params` | `AssociationParams` | what produced it |

**The cost (provisional, recorded with its reason):** for a person `p` of frame `t` and a track `k` whose last person was in frame `t - g`, the time since its last person `<= max_gap_s`:

1. *Boxes*: the box of a person is the hull of its valid keypoints (not the stored box: the stored box depends on the detector cadence and jumps by 10 to 40 % when a detector frame follows derived-box frames). The track's box is its last hull, moved by the predicted mean displacement if prediction is on.
2. *IoU term*: `1 - IoU(hull_p, hull_k)`.
3. *Keypoint term*: the mean distance over the keypoints valid in both (at least `min_common_keypoints`, 4) between the person and the track's (predicted) keypoints, divided by the height of the track's hull (W5: normalised by bbox height). If fewer keypoints are common, the term does not exist.
4. *Combined*: `(w_iou x (1 - IoU) + w_kp x keypoint_term) / (w_iou + w_kp)`; when the keypoint term does not exist: `1 - IoU` alone (the same scale, never a made-up number). `w_iou = w_kp = 1`.
5. *Gate*: a pair with a cost above `max_cost` can never be matched (the value is measured in step 2 on SYN-12 and on the real clip and written with its evidence; placeholder 0.7).

**The assignment:** per frame, the cost matrix of the live tracks against the eligible persons; `scipy.optimize.linear_sum_assignment` on the matrix with gated entries replaced by a large constant; a pair counts only if its true cost is within the gate. Matched persons extend their track; unmatched persons start a new track (ids in row order); unmatched
tracks stay *live* for up to `max_gap_s` seconds (default 0.1 s, from the real `t_sec`: a short missed detection) with their last pose and are then closed. Everything is deterministic: tracks by id, persons by row, ties to the lowest index.

**Eligible persons:** those with at least `raw.params.min_valid_keypoints` valid keypoints and a hull with an area (the rule of P3.04 and of the runner). Weak persons get `track_id = -1`: they cannot steal a match and they do not make ghost tracks.

**Constraints that apply:** AGENTS.md 5.1 (no invented value: `match_cost` is NaN where there is none; the kp term is absent, not 0, when it cannot be computed), 5.2 (real PTS: the prediction uses the real time step), 5.3 (the raw layer is not modified), 5.5 (parameters are provisional and measured, no universal threshold claimed), 6 (units; no `print`; IDs in logs),
9.1 and 9.2 (idea source cited; no code copied), 9.4 point 7 (no dataset content in the repository).

**Unknowns, risks:**

1. *Two people of the same size crossing at the same speed* cannot be told apart by position and pose alone: no appearance feature exists in the MVP. SYN-12 has a scale difference and speed difference; the limit is written in the report and the target choice flags such a take (P3.06 and the UI).
2. *The keypoint term needs good keypoints*: a hidden limb with a low score drops out of the common set; below 4 common keypoints the IoU term decides alone.
3. *Prediction can mislead on an abrupt start or stop of a movement* (Tai Chi is slow and smooth, but the first frame after a pause can be wrong by a frame): the gate and the combined cost limit the damage; measured on the real clip.
4. *A detection gap of more than `max_gap_s`* (an occlusion by another person) starts a new track: P3.06 decides whether to re-attach it to the target.
5. *The gate value* decides between a swap and a lost target; it is a provisional number measured on one real clip and synthetic scenes.

## 4. Implementation log

### Step 2 — the measurement (2026-11-16, scratch prototype of the module with switches; numbers below)

Method: the prototype of `associate.py` (not in the repository) run on SYN-12 (seeds 0 to 9 for each of 8 settings: default, `noise_px` 2 and 4, `walk_px_s` 800, `target_x0_px` 960, `cross_s` 0.5; two settings were refused by the generator) and on five 10 s clips of D20 video 2 (289 frames each, at 120, 300, 470, 660 and 840 s,
saved once with `save_pose_raw`), for five variants: A no prediction, B plain velocity, C velocity damped by 0.8 (displacement capped at half the hull height), D = C with the hull of the keypoints valid in both frames, E = A with that hull.

| Measure | Result |
| ------- | ------ |
| Identity swaps on SYN-12 (sum over 10 seeds, every setting) | **0 for all five variants** (the target has two tracks, before and after its absence: the maximum number of tracks of one identity is 2) |
| Costs on SYN-12, noise 2 px, pooled over 10 seeds: true matches | A: median 0.021, p99 0.106, max 0.116; C: median 0.028, p99 0.063, max 0.110 |
| Costs on SYN-12: wrong pairs (different identities) | A: minimum 0.045, 1st percentile 0.150, median 1.000; C: minimum 0.050 (the smallest wrong pairs are the people at the crossing: the Hungarian assignment, not the gate, separates them) |
| Real clips: the presenter's track covers the clip | 289 of 289 frames in all five clips and all variants |
| Real clips: cost of the presenter's matches (A) | p99 from 0.024 to 0.082, maximum 0.125 (clip 470); with prediction (C) p99 up to 0.151 and maximum 0.183 (clips 660 and 840): the prediction makes the real costs larger, not smaller |
| Real clips: margin of the presenter's matches (A) | 5th percentile 0.62 to 0.67: the nearest competitor costs at least 0.62 more |
| Harder synthetic crossings (two people of equal scale; also 1.0 against 0.95 and 0.98 with noise 1 and 2 px; still against walking, 150 against 400 px/s, 200 and 800 px/s) | when the two bodies coincide in a frame the association there is a tie for every variant (about one identity change per crossing, 9 to 20 per 10 scenes); the prediction does not remove it (B and C: 9 to 20, A: 9 to 20); the smallest `match_margin` at those frames is 0.000 to 0.002 |

Decisions from the numbers: **no motion prediction** (Q4: the simplest variant gives 0 swaps on SYN-12 and an unbroken presenter, the prediction raises the real costs and does not help the exact coincidence); **plain hull** (Q3: the hull of the jointly valid keypoints changes nothing);
**`max_cost = 0.3`** (Q5: the true matches reach 0.125 at most, the nearest wrong match on the real clips costs 0.64 or more; the log-scale middle of 0.125 and 0.64 is 0.28, rounded to 0.3: 2.4 times the largest true cost); **`match_margin` is the honest flag of the unresolvable case**: it is about 0 exactly where two people coincide
and 0.6 or more elsewhere on the real clips. The equal-looking crossing is a limit of the method (no appearance feature), written in the module and the report. Consequences for the plan: T-TRK-02d becomes "the crossing of two identical bodies: `match_margin` flags it, and far from it the margin is large"; the mutants about the prediction are removed; the parameters
`use_velocity`, `velocity_damping`, `max_step_height` and `common_hull` do not exist.

### Hand-over note for P3.06

`associate_tracks(raw)` gives, for every person row, `track_id` (-1: not tracked), `match_cost` and `match_margin` (NaN: none), `gap_frames` and `gap_s` (the time since the previous person of the track: re-attachment must look at the gap), and per track its first and last frame and number of persons. The target is `track_of_person(tracks, select_target(raw).person_row)`.
A target that is absent for more than 0.1 s ends its track and the person who comes back has a **new** track: re-attaching it (cost threshold, `max_lost` of 0.5 s, `target_lost`, asking the user to choose again) is P3.06; `match_cost` between the last pose of the old track and the first pose of the new one is the natural quantity, computed with `_pair_cost` (to be made public by P3.06
if it needs it). `match_margin` near 0 marks places where two people coincide: P3.06 and the UI should say that the identity is uncertain there. Weak persons have no track.
