## 1. Analysis

**Goal:** find how far camera B's timeline is from camera A's at the start and at the end of a take, from the two claps of
each sync signal (capture protocol §5), with ±1 ms precision on clean signals, and say "not found" instead of guessing.

**Facts read (2026-11-15):** W2 steps 1–3 (mono 16 kHz WAV, envelope = absolute value then low-pass, `scipy.signal.correlate`
in a window at the start and at the end, `drift = (offset_end − offset_start) / duration`); T-SYNC-01 (known click offset Δ
found within ±1 ms at 16 kHz), T-SYNC-02 (audio stretched by 0.01 %: drift with the right sign and size), T-SYNC-03 (no click:
"not found", no invented offset). The capture protocol puts ≥ 5 s of recording before the start signal and after the end
signal, two claps about 1 s apart, each with a flash. P2.01 fixes the mapping `t_b = t_a + offset_s + drift × (t_a − t_ref_s)`
on each camera's video timeline.

**Why the A/V start matters:** the audio offset is measured on the audio timelines; the mapping is on the video timelines.
Each file's `audio.start_offset_s` (audio start − video start, from ffprobe, P2.01) converts one to the other. The planning
experiment (synthetic AAC/MP4 from ffmpeg) found audio and video starting together and a click landing within one sample of
its true time; phone files can have encoder priming or edit lists, which P2.05 (flash) and P2.07 (trial) measure.

**In scope:**

1. `io/audio.py`: `extract_audio(recording_dir) -> Path` (`ffmpeg -map 0:a:0 -vn -ac 1 -ar 16000 -c:a pcm_s16le`, atomic,
   cached as `audio.wav`, regenerated if missing), `read_wav(path) -> (samples float32, sr)`.
2. `io/audio_sync.py`: `envelope(x, sr, hp_hz, lp_hz)`, `xcorr_lag(env_a, env_b, sr, max_lag_s) -> LagEstimate`
   (FFT correlation, normalised, parabolic sub-sample peak, `peak`, `peak_ratio`), `estimate_sync(rec_a, rec_b, params) ->
   AudioSync` (start and end windows, offset, drift, `status`), in video time.
3. F-VID fixtures for sync (see the test plan) and tests T-SYNC-01..03 plus robustness cases.

**Out of scope:** flash validation (P2.05), the usability rule and writing `sync.json` and the database row (P2.06),
more than two cameras.

**Design:**

- Envelope: high-pass (removes voice rumble and hum), rectify, low-pass, as W2 says; the cut-offs are engineering parameters
  chosen on the fixtures and written into `params` of `sync.json` (they are not domain thresholds).
- Windows: the start window is the first `w_s` seconds of camera A, matched against the first `w_s + max_lag_s` seconds of B;
  the end window likewise from the end. Defaults `w_s = 30 s`, `max_lag_s = 15 s` (one person presses both phones, so the
  start difference is seconds, not minutes; Q1).
- Found or not: a window is found only when the normalised peak and the peak ratio both pass their minimum; otherwise
  `found = false` and no lag is reported. `status`: `ok` (both), `start_only` (drift `null`), `not_found` (offset `null`).
  No fallback to the phone clock or to zero (AGENTS.md §5.1).
- Drift: `(lag_end − lag_start) / (t_ref_end − t_ref_start)` on camera A's video time; also given in ppm.
- No audio stream in either file: `not_found` with reason `no_audio`.
