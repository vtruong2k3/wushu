## 1. Analysis

**Goal:** fix who does what and when, what it costs, and what the pilot must show, so that gate G05 can say "continue,
change scope, or stop" against numbers decided in advance (G05: "evidence of value by the criteria of P1.08"). The
second half of gate G00 ("a feasible expert schedule") is decided here.

**Agent role:** prepare `Docs/pilot_targets.md` as a template; propose a measurement method for each target; check that
each target has a unit, a baseline, a goal and a method. O and H choose the numbers. The agent does not invent values.

**In scope:**

1. Roles table with pseudonymous IDs (O, A, B, H1, H2, annotator); hours per week; the pilot window and the key dates
   (recording sessions P9.02, coach labelling hours P9.04 and P9.07).
2. Cost summary as totals per role (the unit prices stay in a private file).
3. Pilot targets: a **time** target (for example minutes to review one segment, baseline from P1.03, goal chosen by O)
   and a **review quality** target (for example the share of tool statements that the coach confirms, the share
   corrected or rejected; the exact definitions chosen with H); the stopwatch and counting method; the decision rule
   for G05 written before the pilot.
4. G00 checklist: needs (P1.03), ontology (P1.04, test T-DATA-01), protocol (P1.05), consent (P1.06), trial video
   (P1.09), schedule (this task). If two coaches cannot be secured, the plan says to shrink to a geometry-measurement
   proof of concept. The plan lists G00 twice and the two lists differ: §5 omits consent, §16 includes it and names
   T-DATA-01; this checklist follows §16, the stricter one.

**Out of scope:** running the pilot (P11); choosing thresholds for metrics (not a target of this task).

**Constraints that apply:** AGENTS.md §5.9 (no real names in git: the real names, calendars and unit prices live in a
private file kept by O outside the repository, which the agent never sees); §5.12 (targets about review quality need
the coach).
