## 1. Analysis

**Goal.** The second MVP metric, Appendix B2: the trunk lean in the image plane, `θ(t) = atan2(x_S - x_H, y_H - y_S) - roll_cam` in degrees (0 is vertical, positive when the top of the trunk leans to the image right),
for the rows of a segment, **valid only where the view fits**: the front view for left/right lean and the side view for forward/back lean. The result is the series of `θ`, the numbers drawn from it (the median, and the
median and P95 of `|θ|`), the coverage, the reason of every row without a value, and, when the view does not fit, **no number at all** (T-MET-07: `insufficient_evidence`). The arithmetic of the angle and of the roll was
written and tested in P5.02 (`lean_angle_deg`, `roll_from_line`); this task is the metric around it: the rows, the view and flag conditions, the reasons, the statistics, the summary. No threshold is applied and the
result is `experimental` (AGENTS.md 5.5): the duration over a threshold of B2 exists only with a signed `metric_record`, so it is not produced here.

**Source rules read.** Plan row P5.05 and tests T-MET-06 (SYN-03: `θ` error within 0.5 degrees, golden) and T-MET-07 (unit: a view that does not fit gives `insufficient_evidence` and no number); the tolerance row of SYN-03
and SYN-04 (median `θ` error within 0.5 degrees at noise up to 1 px and a trunk of 200 px, "1 px on 200 px is about 0.4 degrees before the median"); `Docs/TECH_WORKFLOW_V1.md` W7 (only on the intervals whose `view_category`
fits the metric: forward/back lean on the side view, left/right lean on the front view; no universal threshold), Appendix B2 (the formula; the result "median, P95 of `|θ|` and the duration of `|θ|` over the threshold, only with a
`metric_record`"; valid only where the view fits), W3 (the coach chooses `view_category` and the flags `orientation_change`, `occlusion`, `perspective_shift` per segment), section 8.2 and `metric_record` (`supported_views`
is a field of the signed record); v3 6.2 (the axis from the middle of the hips to the middle of the shoulders; the median, the P95 and the duration over a validated threshold); P1.07 (the preliminary card: "valid only on a
front view for left/right lean and a side view for forward/back lean"); P5.01 (the reason 17 `view_not_supported` and 18 `interval_flagged`, and their place in the precedence) and P5.02 to P5.04 (geometry, DEC-0055; the rows,

**Four facts that shape the plan.**

1. **The same angle has two meanings, and the record that will be signed is per meaning.** The formula of B2 is the image-plane angle of the trunk; on a front view it is the left/right lean, on a side view the forward/back lean.
   The `metric_record` is keyed by `(metric_name, version)` and carries `supported_views`, the smallest useful change and the threshold: they differ for the two readings. So there are **two metrics, `trunk_lean_lr` (front) and
   `trunk_lean_fb` (side)**, one function with a `plane` argument (Q1), as variants A and B of the hip height are two metrics (DEC-0014).
2. **The view is a property of the segment, not of the row.** The coach chooses one `view_category` per segment; a performer who turns during the segment is the flag `orientation_change`. So the view gate is the same for every
   row of a series (all rows 17 or none), and a turn inside the segment makes the view assumption false, which is `interval_flagged` (18). There is no per-interval view in the schema and none is invented (Q3).
3. **No scale and no reference here.** An angle does not depend on the trunk length, so there is no `Λ`, no 19 and no 20. What can still take a row out: the coach's intervals (16, optional here: B2 asks only for the view,
   not for a confirmed stable stretch as B1 does) (Q4), the view (17), the flag (18), a missing joint of the four (1 to 6), a trunk of length 0 (the angle is undefined: `frame_invalid`, 4, as P5.02 decided in its Q6) and a run that
   is too short (21).
4. **The series assembly is now needed by a second metric.** P5.03 and P5.04 keep `drop_short_runs`, the masking of the values, `build_series` and the "reason when there is no evidence" inside `hip_height.py`. A third copy
   would drift, so they are lifted into `rows.py` (`assemble_series`, `evidence_reason`) and the 17 gate into `eligibility`, with the hip height variants moved onto them and **no change of their output** (Q10).

**Out of scope.** The duration of `|θ|` over a threshold and any threshold (B2 and AGENTS.md 5.5: only a signed `metric_record`, P5.09 and P10.06); mapping the image sign to the performer's left or right or to forward or back (it needs
which way the performer faces: the report, P8); a per-interval view (not in the segment schema); choosing the camera that shows each plane (P7, P8); circular statistics for a trunk bent past 90 degrees (the wrap at ±180
is not expected for a standing trunk); the speed and pause metrics (P5.06, P5.07).

**Conventions fixed by this task** (each is a place where P5.06, P5.07 and the report could otherwise disagree):

1. **Rows and reasons, in this precedence** (the first that applies): not eligible, 16 outside the coach's intervals (when given), else 17 when the segment's view is not one of the supported views, else 18 when the segment has
   the flag `orientation_change`; then the pose, the reason (1 to 6) of the first of the four joints without a value, in the order hips (11, 12) then shoulders (5, 6); then `frame_invalid` (4) when the four joints have values and the
   two middle points coincide; then 0, measured; then a measured run shorter than `min_valid_s` becomes 21. No 19 and no 20.
2. **The value** is `θ = atan2(x_S - x_H, y_H - y_S) - roll` in degrees, wrapped to (-180, 180], from `lean_angle_deg` (P5.02): positive when the top leans to the image right. It does not depend on the origin, a shift or a zoom of
   the image, and a rotation of the image by the camera roll is taken out by `roll_deg`.
3. **The supported views** are, by default, `front` for `lr` and `side` for `fb`, exactly B2; `oblique`, `back` and `unknown` are not supported until a signed record says so; `supported_views` overrides the default
   (the list of the record) and must be a non-empty list of ontology views; a `view_category` that is not an ontology view is refused (a typo would otherwise be a silent 17).
4. **The statistics** are the signed median of `θ`, the median of `|θ|` and the P95 of `|θ|` (linear interpolation) over the measured rows, in degrees, and `bridged_rows` (measured rows with a bridged hip or shoulder). One
   measured row is enough (a median of one value is that value, and a single row without a step is already 21); no measured row gives `insufficient_evidence` with null statistics and the reason of `evidence_reason` (no row:
   `no_frame`; else the reason with the most rows). Nothing is a threshold: no statistic uses one.
5. **No number is invented**: `min_valid_s` and `supported_views` are arguments; the summary echoes the parameters (`plane`, `roll_deg`, `supported_views` as a short code such as `front` or `front-back`, `min_valid_s`,
   `percentile` 95, `n_intervals`); `experimental`, never conclusive.

**Constraints that apply (AGENTS.md section 5):** 1 no fabricated observation (a row without a joint or with a degenerate trunk has a reason and no value; nothing is filled); 2 real timestamps (the grid of the layer); 3 layers
stay apart; 4 units (degrees) and coordinates (y down, image right is positive) documented; 5 no threshold; 7 no heavy work; 9 nothing personal; 11 no third-party code.

**Unknowns, risks, questions** (answered with the defaults below so that the work can follow; each can be reversed before P5.06 uses it):

- **Q1. One metric or two.** Default: **two metrics, `trunk_lean_lr` and `trunk_lean_fb`**, one function with `plane` (`"lr"` or `"fb"`). Alternative: one metric `trunk_lean` with the plane only in the parameters (one record would then have to
  hold two sets of views and two smallest useful changes).
- **Q2. The default supported views.** Default: **front for left/right, side for forward/back**, as B2 says; oblique, back and unknown give 17 until a signed record extends them. Alternative: also accept `back` for the left/right lean
  (a plausible reading: seen from behind the lateral lean is as visible), which only the coach can decide.
- **Q3. Where the view comes from.** Default: the segment's `view_category` (one per segment); a turn inside the segment is the flag `orientation_change`, which makes every row 18. Alternative: a per-interval view, which needs a
  schema change (a view per stretch of a segment) and a labelling task for the coach.
- **Q4. The coach's intervals.** Default: **optional** (`None` = the whole segment), because B2 asks only for the view; `[]` gives 16 everywhere. Alternative: required as for hip height (B1 asks the coach to confirm a stable stretch; B2 does not).
- **Q5. Which flags block the lean.** Default: **only `orientation_change`** (the view assumption fails when the performer turns). `perspective_shift` (a depth change) does not change an angle; `occlusion` already shows as pose reasons.
- **Q6. The statistics.** Default: the signed median of `θ` (the direction of the habitual lean), the median and the P95 of `|θ|`, and `bridged_rows`; no event; no duration over a threshold. Alternative: only the two statistics of B2
  (median and P95 of `|θ|`), or an event at the largest `|θ|`.
- **Q7. How much evidence.** Default: **one measured row** gives a number (the minimum valid duration is the record's, `min_valid_s`). Alternative: two rows, as the range of hip height needs.
- **Q8. The order of the joints.** Default: hips (11, 12) then shoulders (5, 6), so a row that lacks both gets the hip's reason, as in the hip height metrics; a trunk of length 0 with four joints present is `frame_invalid` (4).
- **Q9. The meaning of the sign.** Default: **image-plane only**: positive when the top leans to the image right; the summary does not say "left" or "right" of the performer or "forward" or "back" (it needs the facing). The report (P8) maps it.
- **Q10. The shared glue.** Default: lift `assemble_series`, `evidence_reason`, the 17 gate and `intervals=None` into `rows.py`, add `MetricResult` to `series.py`, and move the hip height variants onto them with no change of output (the 124 + 70
  tests of P5.03 and P5.04 and an element-by-element comparison with the P5.04 version as the net). Alternative: copy the tail into `trunk_lean.py` (three copies by P5.07).
- **Q11. Estimate.** Default 1 day (raised from 0.5); or 0.5 to 0.75 day if the lift is left for later, which I do not recommend.
- **Q12. A `view_category` that is not an ontology view.** Default: refused with an error naming the views (`VIEWS` is kept equal to the ontology's by a test). Alternative: accepted and unsupported (17).
- *Risk:* the P95 of `|θ|` has a noise floor of about 0.3 degree at 1 px of noise on a 200 px trunk (an upright trunk reads 0.32 at P95); the plan records it and the test bound is 0.5 degree, the tolerance of the plan.
- *Risk:* the angle is only the lean if the performer faces the camera (front) or is side-on (side); a performer turned by some degrees mixes the planes. `orientation_change` is the coach's guard; there is no check from the pose.
- *Risk:* the median of angles is not circular: a trunk bent near ±180 degrees would be misread. Not expected for a standing trunk; stated as a limit.

## 2. Plan

**Design.**

1. `rows.py` (additive; every existing call keeps its meaning).
   - `VIEWS = ("front", "side", "oblique", "back", "unknown")`.
   - `eligibility(t_sec, *, intervals, flags, blocking, view=None, supported_views=None, name="intervals")`: `intervals=None` means every row is inside; with `supported_views` given, `view` must be one of `VIEWS` and the rows
     inside the intervals get 17 when `view` is not in `supported_views` (checked before the flag), else 18 when a blocking flag is set, else 0; `supported_views` is a non-empty list of `VIEWS`; both `view` and `supported_views`
     are given or neither; 16 first, then 17, then 18.
   - `assemble_series(rows, reason, values, *, metric_name, metric_version, units, parameters, min_valid_s, camera_id, view_category) -> MetricSeries`: `drop_short_runs`, the values of demoted rows set to NaN, `build_series`.
   - `evidence_reason(reason, *, min_measured) -> str | None`: `None` when the measured rows reach `min_measured`; else `no_frame` for no row, `too_short` when some but fewer rows were measured, else the leading reason.
2. `series.py`: `MetricResult` (frozen dataclass of `series` and `summary`); `hip_height.py` keeps `HipHeightResult` as a name for it.
3. `hip_height.py`: `_finish` calls `assemble_series`; `_statistics` calls `evidence_reason(reason, min_measured=2)`; nothing else changes.
4. `trunk_lean.py`: `SUPPORTED_VIEWS = {"lr": ("front",), "fb": ("side",)}`, `METRIC_NAMES = {"lr": "trunk_lean_lr", "fb": "trunk_lean_fb"}`, `BLOCKING_FLAGS = ("orientation_change",)`.
   `trunk_lean`: validate `plane`, `min_valid_s`; `rows = segment_rows(layer)`; `eligible = eligibility(rows.t_sec, intervals=intervals, flags=flags, blocking=BLOCKING_FLAGS, view=view_category,
   supported_views=supported_views or SUPPORTED_VIEWS[plane], name="intervals")`; `pose = unusable_reason(kp, reason, (11, 12, 5, 6))`; `theta = lean_angle_deg(mid_hip(kp), mid_shoulder(kp), roll_deg)` (computed on every row, which
   also validates the roll whether or not a row is measured); `reason = where(eligible, eligible, where(pose, pose, where(isnan(theta), 4, 0)))`; the values are `theta` on the measured rows; `assemble_series`;
   statistics from the measured rows; `make_summary(..., record_status="experimental")`.
5. Errors are `MetricError` naming the argument: not a `PoseFiltered`, a `plane` that is not `lr` or `fb`, a `view_category` that is not an ontology view, a `supported_views` that is empty or has a name that is not a view, bad
   intervals, a flag that is not an ontology flag or not a `bool`, a roll beyond 45 degrees or not a number, a minimum that is not `None` or a finite number above 0, a `camera_id` the header refuses.
