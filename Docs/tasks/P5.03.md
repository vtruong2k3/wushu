## 1. Analysis

**Goal.** The first of the three MVP metrics, variant A of Appendix B1: `h_A(t) = y_H(t) / Λ_take` for the rows of a segment, where `y_H` is the vertical image position of the middle of the hips (after the
camera roll is taken out) and `Λ_take` is the trunk length measured on the take's **reference standing segment** and kept fixed. The result is `range = P95(h_A) - P5(h_A)` over the valid rows, with the profile (the
series), the coverage, the reason of every row that has no value, and the instants of the extremes. This is also the first metric that reads a `PoseFiltered`, so the few lines that cut the rows of a segment out of
it (P5.02 left them to this task) are written here, once, for P5.04 to P5.07 to reuse. No threshold is applied and the result is `experimental` (AGENTS.md 5.5): the value is shown and nothing is concluded.

**Source rules read.** Plan row P5.03 and tests T-MET-03 (SYN-02: variant A, `range ≈ 2A` within 5 %), row SYN-02 of the tolerances (the continuous answer is 0.9877 x 2A, so about 3.8 % is left for smoothing and
sampling); `Docs/TECH_WORKFLOW_V1.md` W7 (pixels, y down; no universal threshold; the notes of P5.01 and P5.02) and Appendix B1 (`h_A = y_H / Λ_take` with Λ_take from the reference standing segment; result `P95 - P5`
over the valid interval with profile, coverage and extreme instants; conditions: fixed camera, a segment the coach confirms as "expected stable height", no large depth change = flag `perspective_shift`); v3 6.1 and
8.2 to 8.3 (the minimum valid duration belongs to the signed `metric_record`; three coverage ratios); DEC-0014 (A and B are two metrics, no order asserted); `Docs/capture_protocol.md` section 5 (the performer stands
still 3 to 5 s before the form: that is the reference); P5.01 (the codes 16 to 22 and their precedence) and P5.02 (geometry, DEC-0055).

**Existing code read.** `metrics/geometry.py` (`mid_hip`, `unusable_reason`, `bridged`, `trunk_length`, `trunk_scale`, `correct_roll`); `metrics/series.py` (`build_series`, `make_summary`); `schemas/metric.py` (`MissingReason`,
`Statistic`, `MetricEvent`, units, `parameters`); `quality/layers_io.py` (`PoseFiltered`: `t_grid`, `keypoints`, `reason`, `interpolated`, `grid_in_segment`, `meta.timeline.step_s`, `meta.recording_id`,

`config/ontology_v0.yaml` and `schemas/ontology.py` (the flags `orientation_change`, `occlusion`, `perspective_shift`); the generator (`tests/helpers/synth.py`: SYN-02, SYN-09 and their truth).

**Three facts that shape the plan.**

1. **The reference standing segment is not in the layer of the segment.** The pose pass of a segment reads `[start - 1 s, end + 1 s]` only (`PoseRunParams.segment_margin_s`), so a `PoseFiltered` holds one segment and
   its margins. The standing reference is a segment of its own, with its own pose pass and its own `PoseFiltered` (of the same recording: one stored video, hence one camera). `Λ_take` is therefore computed from the
   **`grid_in_segment` rows of the reference layer**, and `hip_height_a` takes that layer as an argument (Q1).
2. **The reasons already exist and have a fixed order.** P5.01 gave a code to every outcome this metric can have: 16 `outside_interval` (the coach did not confirm the row), 18 `interval_flagged` (`perspective_shift`),
   1 to 6 the pose reasons of the first unusable hip, 19 `scale_unavailable`, 20 `reference_missing`, 21 `too_short`; and the order in which they count (not eligible, then pose, then good pose but not measured).
   17 `view_not_supported` is not used by A (Q5). So the work is to apply that order, not to invent reasons.
3. **No number is invented.** Appendix B1 fixes the percentiles (5 and 95) and the roll; it does not fix a minimum reference length or a minimum valid run: v3 8.2 puts "minimum valid duration" into the signed
   `metric_record` (P5.09), which does not exist yet. Both minimums are therefore **optional arguments, `None` = none applied**, echoed in the `parameters` of the summary (Q2).

**Conventions fixed by this task** (each is a place where P5.04 to P5.07 could otherwise disagree):

1. **A series row is a row of `grid_in_segment`** (closed, from the first to the last row of the segment, as `quality.json` counts it); the margins are never in a series. `t_sec` is `t_grid`; `step_s` is
   `meta.timeline.step_s`.
2. **`h_A` is the image position of the hip, y down**: `h_A = y'_H / Λ_take` with `y'` the second coordinate of `correct_roll(mid_hip, roll_deg)` with the origin `(0, 0)`. At a roll of 0 it is exactly `y_H / Λ_take` of
   Appendix B1 and it grows when the hip goes **down** the image, so the event `max` is the lowest hip. The absolute level depends on the origin and on where the camera looks, so only differences mean anything:
   the statistic is the range, and no P5 or P95 level is reported.
3. **Reasons of a row, in this precedence** (the first that applies): not eligible (16, then 18) → hip unusable (`unusable_reason` of joints 11 and 12: the first of them without a value, 1 to 6) → no reference (20) →
   scale unavailable (19) → measured (0) → then a measured run shorter than `min_valid_s` becomes 21. A bridged hip has a value and is measured; it is counted in `bridged_rows` (P5.02 Q1).
4. **`Λ_take`** is `trunk_scale(trunk_length(keypoints))` over the reference layer's `grid_in_segment` rows (P5.02), taken as unavailable (19) when there is no valid row or the rows last less than
   `min_reference_s`; the reference must be of the **same `recording_id`** as the layer and carry the same `data_sources` (a scale from another video is refused, not used).
5. **The range** is `np.percentile(h, 95) - np.percentile(h, 5)` (linear interpolation, as the generator's truth) over the measured rows, and needs **at least two measured rows**; fewer is `insufficient_evidence`
   (no number, never 0). The reason a summary gives without evidence: no row at all → `no_frame`; exactly one measured row → `too_short`; otherwise the reason with the most rows among the missing ones (the smaller
   code on a tie).
6. **Stable intervals are an explicit argument** and `[start, end)` in stream seconds; an empty list is allowed and makes every row 16. `flags` is the segment's flag mapping (`{}` = none set); only
   `perspective_shift` blocks hip height (B1); a name that is not an ontology flag is refused.

**Constraints that apply (AGENTS.md section 5):** 1 no fabricated observation (a row without a hip has no value; a range is never computed from fewer than two rows; no fill); 2 real timestamps (`t_grid`, the
step is the layer's); 3 layers stay apart (the metric reads the final layer and writes its own objects); 4 units and coordinates documented, the metric carries version, units, intervals, coverage and reasons;
5 no threshold (the minimums are arguments, the result is `experimental`); 7 no heavy work; 9 nothing personal (a pseudonymous `recording_id`, `camera_id`); 11 no third-party code.

**Unknowns, risks, questions** (answered with the defaults below so that the work can follow; each can be reversed before P5.04 uses it):

- **Q1. Where does Λ_take come from?** Default: **the `PoseFiltered` of the reference standing segment, same recording**, passed as `reference` (fact 1). Alternative: widen the pose pass of the segment to reach back
  to the reference (a pose-stage change: it costs GPU time on every segment and changes P3 and P7).
- **Q2. Minimums.** Default: `min_reference_s` and `min_valid_s` are optional arguments, `None` = none, echoed in `parameters`; the signed record (P5.09) supplies them. Alternative: a built-in default now (for
  example 1 s): that is a universal threshold by another name (AGENTS.md 5.5).
- **Q3. Stable intervals.** Default: an **explicit argument** (the coach's "expected stable height" intervals); an empty list gives only `outside_interval`. Alternative: default to the whole segment, which would claim a
  confirmation nobody gave.
- **Q4. Which flags block hip height?** Default: **only `perspective_shift`** (B1). `occlusion` already shows as pose reasons; `orientation_change` does not move the hips' image height.
- **Q5. Views.** Default: **any view is eligible** for hip height (W7 names views only for the lean); reason 17 stays for P5.05 and for the supported views of a future record.
- **Q6. Extremes.** Default: events `max` and `min` at the **first** row of the largest and the smallest `h_A` among the measured rows (the series is already filtered). Alternative: events from a smoothed profile
  (a second filter on a filtered series: not now).
- **Q7. Origin and absolute level.** Default: origin `(0, 0)`, no level reported (convention 2). Alternative: the image centre (needs the image size at every call; no range depends on it).
- **Q8. Where the glue lives.** Default: `metrics/rows.py`, shared. Alternative: private helpers in `hip_height.py` that P5.05 would have to lift (a second change of a file that is DONE).
- **Q9. A segment with no rows** (`grid_in_segment` all false): default **an `insufficient_evidence` summary with reason `no_frame`**, not an exception (a pipeline can report it). A single row has no step: it gets 21.
- **Q10. Estimate.** Default 1 day (raised from 0.5) with the glue; or split into P5.03a (`rows.py` with its tests) and P5.03b (`hip_height.py`) if you prefer two closes.
- *Risk:* the camera of the reference is checked only through `recording_id` (one stored video = one camera); P7 must pass the layer of the same recording, and its contract test will say so.
- *Risk:* a reference in which the performer is not still shrinks or stretches Λ unnoticed (no threshold to catch it); the coach's label and the audit P5.10 are the control. The summary echoes `scale_px`,
  `reference_rows` and the reference duration so a reviewer can see them.
- *Risk:* SYN-09 asks for 0.02 Λ on the range; Λ_take comes from 30 rows with 1 px of noise (0.5 %): the first measurement fixes what is achievable and the test keeps the plan's tolerance.

## 2. Plan

**Design.**

1. `metrics/rows.py`: pure functions plus one frozen dataclass.
   `segment_rows(layer: PoseFiltered) -> SegmentRows` (rows of `grid_in_segment`, contiguous: refused otherwise; `N = 0` allowed; arrays copied and read-only; `MetricError` for a non-`PoseFiltered`).
   `interval_mask(t_sec, intervals)`: `(t >= start - 1e-9) & (t < end - 1e-9)` per interval, or-ed; each interval two finite numbers with `start < end`, else `MetricError` naming the index.
   `eligibility(t_sec, *, intervals, flags, blocking)`: `uint8` 0, 16 (outside every interval) or 18 (inside, a blocking flag set); `flags` must map ontology flag names to `bool` (`FLAGS` is the closed list of
   `ontology_v0`; a test keeps it equal to `load_ontology().flags`).
   `drop_short_runs(reason, *, step_s, min_valid_s)`: a new array where each maximal run of rows with reason 0 whose duration `rows x step_s` is below `min_valid_s` becomes 21; `None` changes nothing; with
   `step_s is None` (fewer than two rows) every measured row becomes 21.
   `leading_reason(reason)`: the `MissingReason` name that a summary without evidence gives (convention 5).
2. `metrics/hip_height.py`:
   `ReferenceScale(scale_px, n_rows, duration_s)` and `reference_scale(reference, *, min_reference_s=None)`: `segment_rows`, `trunk_scale(trunk_length(...))`, duration `n_rows x step_s` (0 when there is no step);
   `scale_px` is NaN (rows kept) when the minimum is not reached.
   `hip_height_a(...)`: validate the arguments; `rows = segment_rows(layer)`; Λ and its row reason (20 if `reference is None`, else 19 when Λ is NaN); `eligibility`; `unusable_reason(kp, reason, HIP_JOINTS)`; compose the reason
   with the precedence of convention 3; `values = correct_roll(mid_hip(kp), roll)[:, 1] / Λ` on measured rows, NaN elsewhere; `drop_short_runs`; `build_series` (name `hip_height_a`, version `1`, units
   `body_lengths`, the step and `data_sources` of the layer, `parameters`); statistics and events; `make_summary(..., record_status="experimental")`.
3. `parameters` of the header (all bounded, no NaN): `variant` `"a"`, `roll_deg`, `min_reference_s`, `min_valid_s`, `percentile_low` 5, `percentile_high` 95, `scale_px` (null when unavailable), `reference_rows`,
   `reference_s`, `n_stable_intervals`.
4. Errors are `MetricError` naming the argument: not a `PoseFiltered`, different `recording_id` or `data_sources` between layer and reference, bad intervals, a flag that is not an ontology flag or not a `bool`, a
   roll beyond 45 degrees, a minimum that is not `None` or a finite number above 0, a `camera_id` or `view_category` the header refuses.
