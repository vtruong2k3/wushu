## 1. Analysis

**Goal:** the sixth step of the quality layer (W6): "left/right swap: detect the simultaneous jump of an L/R pair, flag `lr_swap_suspect`; the MVP does not correct, it warns". The pose model sometimes gives the
label `left` to the right limb and the reverse. P3.08 found 11 such frames in 250 (8 in ordinary frames): legs and feet in side and oblique lunges of `d20-v4`, one pair of legs in `d20-v1`, one ankle in `d20-v2`,
labels that flip between frames 0.3 s apart; the score does not warn (DEC-0042), and Hampel does not see a swap that lasts (every sample of its window shows it, DEC-0046). This task reads the **mask layer** (observed cells,
before Hampel) and marks, for every pair of `LR_PAIRS`, the rows at which **both labels jump onto each other's previous place** and the rows between such a jump and the one that undoes it. It **changes no value** (AGENTS.md 5.1: to swap
the labels back would change what a measurement means). The mask it produces is the `blocked` input of the gap layer ("do not bridge across a suspected turning point", W6; DEC-0045), and the table of events feeds the
warnings of the report (`lr_swap_suspect@12.40-12.73`, TECH_WORKFLOW).

**What the analysis measured (numbers only, nothing committed; D20 pass of P3.08, N = 1, 22 chunks, 158,326 rows, mask layer):**

- **The event.** For a pair `(a, b)` at row `t`, with the four cells observed at `t - 1` and `t` at most 0.25 s apart, and distances in torso lengths: both labels moved at least `J` (`stay_a`, `stay_b`) and each one landed within
  `rho` times its own move of the **other** label's previous place (`cross_a = |a_t - b_{t-1}|`, `cross_b = |b_t - a_{t-1}|`). A fast limb that does not cross, and two limbs that cross smoothly, do not satisfy it (a label that
  moves 0.1 to 0.2 torso per frame is below `J`, and a smooth crossing has no jump). The existing preview of this idea, `audit/signals.swap_margin` ((stay - cross) over body height, the maximum over the pairs), had a precision
  of 40 % in P3.08 as a ranking rule; it is superseded here and left as it is (the P3.08 results stay reproducible).
- **Where the events are.** At `J` = 0.2 torso and `rho` = 0.5: **270 events (17.1 per 10,000 rows), all of them in `d20-v4`** (the lunges seen from the side; none in the other three videos, one in `d20-v3` at `J` = 0.1);
  by pair: eyes 0, ears 4, shoulders 8, elbows 6, wrists 9, hips 38, **knees 53, ankles 56, big toes 14, small toes 30, heels 52** (205 of the 270 are in the leg chain); 62 rows have two or more pairs jumping at once. The count hardly
  moves with the threshold: 278 events at `J` = 0.1, 270 at 0.2, 240 at 0.3, 217 at 0.5 (`rho` 0.5; 245 to 214 at `rho` 0.25), so the events are big jumps and not jitter.
- **The labelled swaps.** 11 frames of `lr_swap` (8 ordinary), 104 labelled cells that belong to a pair. An event of the labelled pair lies within +-2 rows of **69 of the 104 cells** and of **9 of the 11 frames (6 of the 8 ordinary)**;
  the two that are not covered (`fc-014`, legs in `d20-v1`, and `fc-056`, one ankle in `d20-v2`) have no event of that pair anywhere in their chunk: a swap that is not a jump between consecutive rows is not seen. Of the **172 frames
  that a person confirmed right**, **2** have an event within +-2 rows (3 at `J` = 0.1). **Caveat: this is not an independent measurement.** The frames to review were chosen by rules, among them `swap_pair`, which is a preview of this very
  event; the nearest event to 9 of the 11 labelled frames is 0 to 0.07 s away because the review looked at those jumps. The numbers say the detector sees what the review saw, not how many swaps there are.
- **Time between events.** Of 207 consecutive event pairs of one pair-label (`J` 0.2, `rho` 0.5): 60 within 0.1 s, 88 within 0.25 s, 98 within 0.5 s, 108 within 1 s, 147 within 5 s, 60 longer. About half are a jump and the jump back within a quarter of
  a second (a swap lasting a few frames, as SYN-08); the others are alone, or the swap lasts or flips again and again. Pairing an event with the next one of the same pair within 0.5 s and flagging the rows between them covers
  **6 of the 11 labelled frames** (the frame of the undoing jump is the first row that is no longer swapped, and the labelled frames are mostly rows of a jump): so the **rows of the events themselves** have to be marked too, which
  covers 9 of 11. The share of rows flagged is 0.07 % (pairing within 0.25 s) to 0.11 % (0.5 s) to 0.19 % (1 s) of all rows.

**What is not obvious, and why this plan asks questions:**

- **A swap of two joints that are close together changes nothing a metric can see**, and the detector cannot see it either (the jump is smaller than `J`): the eyes, ears and toes of a front view are never flagged. That is intended: the
  flag is for swaps that move a position by more than `J` torso lengths.
- **The state of a swap is not observed, only its jumps.** Between a jump in and a jump out the labels are exchanged; with one jump alone (the swap lasts to the end of the run, or the other jump was missed) the duration is unknown. The
  rule that follows pairs events greedily in time order within a limit and says so in the table; it can be wrong when a jump is missed or invented (the parity of the flips is lost for the rest of a long swap).
- **The mask layer, not the Hampel layer.** A swap of one to three rows is a spike of both labels: Hampel flags it as outliers and the gap layer bridges it, which repairs it by accident and hides it. The swap flag must see the observed
  positions, so it reads the mask layer; the order of the stages stays mask, Hampel, gaps (the flag is computed beside Hampel, not after it) and P4.07 wires it.

**The rules** (every rule has a test sub-ID below):

1. **The scale.** `torso` is `torso_px` or the median torso of the layer (the distance between the middle of the shoulders and the middle of the hips over the rows with the four joints observed). Every distance below is in torso lengths.
   No torso: no event.
2. **The event.** A pair `(a, b)` has an event at row `t >= 1` if the cells `a` and `b` are observed at `t - 1` and at `t`, `t_sec[t] - t_sec[t-1] <= max_dt_s` (+1e-9 s), and with `stay_a = |a_t - a_{t-1}|`, `stay_b = |b_t - b_{t-1}|`,
   `cross_a = |a_t - b_{t-1}|`, `cross_b = |b_t - a_{t-1}|`: `stay_a >= min_jump`, `stay_b >= min_jump`, `cross_a <= max_cross_ratio x stay_a` and `cross_b <= max_cross_ratio x stay_b` (the limits are inclusive). Both labels must
   jump, and each must land near the other one's previous place: a limb that moves fast alone, a smooth crossing, and a label that jumps alone (a flicker: Hampel's) are not events.
3. **Pairing.** The events of one pair, in time order, are taken greedily: an event is the **enter** of a swap if the next event of the same pair is at most `max_swap_s` later (+1e-9 s); that one is its **exit**, and both are consumed. An event
   without a partner is **unpaired**. The rows from the enter (inclusive) to the exit (exclusive) are the swapped rows: `suspect` is True for the **two joints of the pair** in those rows. An unpaired event marks no interval.
4. **The rows of the events are marked too.** `events` is True at every event row, for the pair; `blocked` is `suspect` plus the two joints of every event row (the jump itself is a row at which the labels are not to be trusted), and the exit row is
   in it although the labels there are right again.
5. **Nothing is changed or filled.** No value, reason or score is changed; a cell that is not observed is never part of an event; a swap across a hole (the labels are not observed in the rows that matter) is not seen.
6. **Real time.** The limits `max_dt_s` and `max_swap_s` are in seconds of the real timestamps; no frame rate is assumed.

**Unknowns, risks, questions** (the maintainer is asked; the proposed answer is applied if there is no other):

- **Q1. The rule.** **Proposed: the crossed jump of a pair** (rule 2) with `min_jump` = 0.2 torso and `max_cross_ratio` = 0.5, per pair (not several pairs at once). The events hardly depend on the values (278 to 217 events for `J` 0.1 to 0.5, 245 to 214 for `rho`
  0.25 against 0.5); 0.2 torso is about 38 px on D20, a jump no jitter makes. Alternatives: the existing `swap_margin` (a ranking signal with 40 % precision); a constant-velocity prediction of each label (more machinery, more parameters, no data that it is better);
  requiring two or more pairs at once (a single ankle swap, `fc-056`, would be lost; 62 of the 101 event rows have two or more pairs).
- **Q2. What is flagged.** **Proposed: a warning only, in two masks:** `suspect` (the two joints of the pair between an enter and its exit) and `events` (the jump rows); `blocked` is their union, for the gap layer; no label is swapped back.
  Alternatives: also swap the labels back in the suspect interval (a correction: the MVP says warn; it would change what a measurement is, and a wrong parity would swap right labels); flag every joint of the frame (too coarse: a leg swap would blame the arms).
- **Q3. Pairing.** **Proposed: greedy in time order, `max_swap_s` = 0.5 s,** an event with no partner marks its row only (`unpaired`). Of the 207 consecutive events 98 are within 0.5 s of the next; the rest are alone or part of a long or repeated swap. Alternatives:
  a shorter limit (0.25 s: 88 pairs), a longer one (1 s: 108); mark a margin of rows around every event instead of an interval (it does not need the parity but cannot tell the swapped rows from the others).
- **Q4. The scale.** **Proposed: the median torso of the layer** (`torso_length_px`), with `torso_px` as an override; P4.07 can pass a torso measured on the whole take. Alternative: the torso of each frame (noisy where a shoulder or hip is missed).
- **Q5. Where in the chain.** **Proposed: the flag reads the mask layer (before Hampel) and is computed beside it; P4.07 passes `blocked` to the gap layer and keeps both stages as they are.** Alternative: after Hampel (a one-to-three-row swap would then already have been
  turned into outlier holes and bridged: invisible).
- **Risk: the sensitivity is not known.** The 11 labelled frames were chosen by rules that include a preview of this detector; the coverage of 9 of 11 (6 of 8 ordinary) is what the review saw. The blind sample of P3.08 (40 frames no rule pointed at, 2 failures) cannot say more.
  The plan reports it as it is and does not claim a recall.
- **Risk: false alarms on fast movement.** A genuine crossing of two limbs at more than about 0.2 torso per frame each, in opposite directions, can look like a crossed jump (about 900 px/s at 24 fps on D20). 2 of 172 confirmed-right frames have an event nearby;
  the probe could not say whether those two are real swaps that the reviewers missed.
- **Risk: the parity of the intervals.** A missed or invented event turns the swapped rows of the rest of a long swap into unflagged rows and the reverse; the unpaired outcome and the limit `max_swap_s` bound the damage to a pair of events, and `events` and `blocked` mark every jump anyway.
- **Risk: cost.** Vector operations over `(T, P)` arrays and one loop over the events of each pair; no per-cell Python loop.

## 2. Plan

**Design:**

```python
# quality/lr_swap.py
ENTER, EXIT, UNPAIRED = 0, 1, 2
EVENT_OUTCOME_NAMES: Final = ("enter", "exit", "unpaired")      # the index is the code

@dataclass(frozen=True)
class SwapParams:
    min_jump: float = 0.2             # [torso] > 0, finite: both labels move at least this far
    max_cross_ratio: float = 0.5      # (0, 1]: each lands within this share of its own move of the other label's previous place
    max_dt_s: float = 0.25            # [s] > 0, finite: the two rows are at most this far apart
    max_swap_s: float = 0.5           # [s] > 0, finite: an event is paired with the next one of its pair within this time
    pairs: tuple[tuple[int, int], ...] = LR_PAIRS     # distinct joints in [0, 25], no joint twice

@dataclass(frozen=True, eq=False)
class EventTable: pair, row, t_s, jump, outcome       # one row per event, ordered by pair then row

@dataclass(frozen=True, eq=False)
class SwapResult:                                      # events, suspect, table, torso_px, params; properties blocked, frame_suspect

def detect_lr_swaps(keypoints, reason, t_sec, params=None, *, torso_px=None) -> SwapResult
def detect_lr_swaps_track(masked: MaskedTrack, params=None, *, torso_px=None) -> SwapResult
```

Data flow: `check_layer` → the scale (`torso_px` or `torso_length_px`) → for every pair the arrays `a`, `b` of shape `(T, 2)`, the observed masks, the real time step; rows `1..T-1` get `stay`, `cross` and the event mask by array operations → the events of a pair in time order →
the greedy pairing (a loop over the events of a pair, not over cells) → `suspect` by filling the rows of each pair of events for the pair's two joints → `blocked` and `frame_suspect` as properties → read-only result and the table. No value of the input appears in the result.
