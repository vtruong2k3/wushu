## 1. Analysis

**Goal:** the access requests that take weeks to answer are sent early, so that data can arrive when P10 needs it, and
the registry shows what was sent and when. Gate G01 counts this task (plan §3: "G01 together with P1.10").

**Hard rule:** AGENTS.md §9.4.1: the agent never submits a request form or sends an email; the maintainer decides
whether to apply. The agent prepares and records.

**Facts from `Docs/DATASET_REGISTRY_TAIJI.md` (read 2026-11-15):**

| Dataset | How to ask | What it is for | Allowed use now |
| ------- | ---------- | -------------- | --------------- |
| D11 PSU-TMM100 | Dataset Request Form (link in the registry); conditions from the provider (IRB study); a citation is mandatory | check the 2D metrics against motion capture (P10.07, optional) | `not-usable` until granted; then `benchmark` as planned |
| D06 TaiChi-AQA | request form file named in the project README, sent by email | 24-form recognition and quality (extensions E01, E03) | `not-usable` until granted, then as the agreement says |
| D10 TaiChi-24 | sign an agreement and email | same | `not-usable` until granted, then as the agreement says |

D11 is the only one that serves the MVP (metric validation). D06 and D10 serve later extensions after G05, so sending
them now creates obligations earlier than the need.

**In scope:**

1. A short checklist per dataset: what to send, to whom (from the registry), what each form asks, what the data may be
   used for, which citation is required.
2. A draft of the intended-use paragraph that matches the registry's allowed use and states the project truthfully
   (internal measurement tool, development and benchmark); the wording and the statements about commercial plans are
   O's.
3. After O sends: update `DATASET_REGISTRY_TAIJI.md` with the date and the status "request sent" for each dataset; the
   allowed use stays `not-usable` until access is granted.

**Out of scope:** downloading anything, mirrors of unknown origin (AGENTS.md §9.1), using the data before the grant and
only as the agreement allows (§9.4), E01/E03 work.

**Risks:** a provider may require an academic affiliation or an institutional signature that an individual or company
requester cannot give; the answer can take weeks or never come. The plan does not depend on it (D11 is optional,
P10.07).
