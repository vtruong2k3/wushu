## 1. Analysis

**Goal:** one document, `Docs/metric_definitions_v0.md`, that says for each kept metric what it measures, how, on which
views, under which conditions, and what it cannot tell. P5 implements it, P10.02 checks it against hand measurements,
and the coach signs that the definition means what the coach means.

**Sources:** `TECH_WORKFLOW_V1.md` Appendix B (formulas B1–B3), §3 W7 (view rules, no universal thresholds), decision 3
and 4 of §12; `DEC-0014` (both hip-height variants are computed and reported, chosen by error against hand
measurements); `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN_V1.md` §14.3 (the F-SYN scenarios SYN-02..SYN-05 and their tolerances).

**In scope:** per metric a card with these fields: question it answers for the coach (wording from P1.03), definition
and formula (pixel image coordinates, y down), joints used (HALPE_26 indexes), units, required `view_category` and
flags, preconditions, outputs (series and summary), limits (single camera, 2D, no depth), what is not claimed, status
`experimental` with the `metric_record` fields left empty (no threshold now), the hand-measurement idea for P10.02, and
the questions for H. A plain-language summary per metric for H.

**Out of scope:** thresholds, "good" and "bad" values (AGENTS.md §5.5: only a signed `metric_record` may hold them, in
P10.06); implementation (P5); the choice between hip-height variants A and B (DEC-0014 keeps both).

**Metrics (from the plan, to be confirmed by P1.03):** hip-height variation (B1, variants A and B); trunk lean in the
image plane (B2, valid only on a front view for left/right lean and a side view for forward/back lean); tempo and
continuity (B3, group speed in body lengths per second, pauses, cut-off ablation 4/6/8 Hz).

**Constraints that apply:** AGENTS.md §5.4 (units and coordinates stated), §5.5, §5.12 (the coach signs), §5.1 (no
filled-in values).

**Risk:** P1.03 can change the list; the document waits for it. The plan's week 1 schedules P1.07 together with
P1.01–P1.03, which is only realistic if the interviews finish early in the week.
