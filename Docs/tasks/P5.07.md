## 1. Analysis

**Goal.** Appendix B3 of `TECH_WORKFLOW_V1.md`: *a pause is an interval with `V_G < τ` lasting at least `d_min`; `τ` and `d_min` are learned from the coach's labels for the same kind of movement, never a common value*. The result of B3 adds the number and the lengths of the pauses to the profile `V_G` that P5.06 already gives. The plan row says: `τ` and `d_min` come from a configuration, and **without them only the profile is output**. This task adds the detection of the pauses on the profile, as an optional part of `group_speed` and as a pure function on a series, with the numbers (count, total, longest, those cut by a hole) and one `MetricEvent` of kind `pause` per pause. It adds no number of its own: `τ` and `d_min` are arguments (`None` = no pause detection), the output is `experimental` and labelled with the threshold source `unsigned_config`, never conclusive (AGENTS.md 5.5). The ablation of the cut-off at 4, 6 and 8 Hz is P5.08 and runs this on other layers; the signed record that will supply the thresholds is P5.09.

**Source rules read.** Plan row P5.07 and tests T-MET-09 (SYN-05: the right number of pauses, boundaries within 1 frame) and T-MET-10 (SYN-01 and SYN-06: no false pause from noise or a gap); the SYN-05 row of the tolerances ("the right number of pauses; each boundary within 1 frame of the cut-off threshold τ (noise-free)"; "the generator gives the continuous speed profile"); `TECH_WORKFLOW_V1.md` Appendix B3 and W7; v3 6.3 (the thresholds and the minimum pause are learned from the coach's labels for the same kind of movement; the rhythm is judged on the original time axis); `Docs/tasks/P5.06.md` (the profile, the noise floor of 0.07 Λ/s, the conventions that P5.07 reads); `schemas/metric.py` (`MetricEvent`: a pause is a stretch with `t_start < t_end`, `value` null and `uses_threshold` true; every event lies inside a valid interval; a threshold output needs a `threshold_source`, `unsigned_config` only while `experimental`); DEC-0054 (the threshold rules), DEC-0059 (the profile and its conventions); M-QLT-05 (the filter's effect on the speed in a pause).

**Existing code read.** `metrics/tempo.py` (`group_speed`, the statistics, the parameters echoed); `metrics/series.py` (`make_summary(events=, threshold_source=)`, `build_series`, `check_series`); `metrics/rows.py` (`assemble_series`, `minimum_s`, `evidence_reason`); `schemas/metric.py` (above); the generator (`tests/helpers/synth.py`: `_motion`, the cosine ramps of `_speed_factor`, `speed_at`, the truth `pause_intervals`, `pause_frames`, `n_pauses`); the SYN-05, SYN-06, SYN-07 and SYN-01 builders and their defaults (`_MOTION`: `raise_lower`, ramp 0.2 s; SYN-05 pauses (1.0, 1.6) and (2.8, 3.4) in 5 s; SYN-07 one pause (1.0, 1.5) in 4 s; SYN-06 gaps of the wrists at 0.3 s for 300 ms and at 2.0 s for 100 ms; SYN-01 still, `n_pauses` 1 over the whole clip).

**Five facts that shape the plan.**

1. **A pause is only as good as `τ` is above the noise floor.** The speed of a joint that does not move reads 0.06 to 0.18 Λ/s at 1 px of noise on a 200 px trunk (P5.06). A threshold at or under that floor fragments a real pause or never sees one; a threshold well above it (0.3 Λ/s here, 1.7 times the largest value seen) gives one pause for the still clip. P5.07 uses no default `τ` and the tests that use noise choose `τ` above the floor and say why; the lesson for the signed record is written in section 5.
2. **The turning point of a movement is a slow stretch.** The speed of a movement that goes out and comes back is 0 at its apex. That stretch is a pause in the sense `V < τ` unless `d_min` removes it. B3's `d_min` is what separates a pause that the performer makes from a turning point, and it has to be learned from the coach's labels for the kind of movement; the generator's `raise_lower` shows the need (0.23 s of turning point against 0.6 s pauses).
3. **The boundary of a pause is resolved to about half a step above 10 % of the peak, and worse below it.** The 1-frame tolerance of the plan holds at `τ` = 10 % of the peak (0.51 to 0.53 steps) and not at 5 % (1.05 to 1.07): the plan's tolerance is noise-free and for a reasonable `τ`; the number at 5 % is recorded, not hidden.
4. **A pause is a stretch of measured rows only.** A row that is not measured has no speed, so it is neither slow nor fast: a pause is never extended or joined across it, and a hole cuts a pause short (the measurement above: a 0.7 s pause seen as 0.2 s). The honest answer is to say so: a pause that touches a row lost by the pose is **cut**, its length is a lower bound, and the summary counts how many are. A hole at the edge of the segment, at the coach's interval or at the end of the track is a boundary of the protocol, not a loss.
5. **The truth of the pauses is the continuous profile, not the generator's `pause_intervals`.** The generator's exact pauses are where the speed is 0; the stretches where `V < τ` are slightly longer (the ramps) and include the turning point. The truth for T-MET-09 is therefore the set of intervals where the analytic speed is below `τ`, which `speed_at` can give; a helper `slow_intervals(seq, group, tau)` in the generator, with its own test against the exact pauses at a very small `τ`, makes that truth public instead of being rebuilt in each test.

**Out of scope.** The values of `τ` and `d_min` (the signed record, P5.09, from the coach's labels of P9; no default here); the ablation of the cut-off (P5.08); hysteresis, merging of pauses that are close, a filter on `V` (B3 gives the plain definition; each would be a new parameter); a pause of the whole body (B3 defines it per group); the pause's position in the form or its comparison with a reference (W8); a threshold on the length of a pause ("too long", "too short": the coach's, P10); the schema (`MetricEvent` already has the pause kind; no field is added: a "cut" flag as a field would be a schema change, see Q5); any wiring in a worker or a file (P7).

**Conventions fixed by this task** (each is a place where P5.08, the record and the report could otherwise disagree):

1. **A pause** is a maximal run of consecutive rows of the series that are **measured** (reason 0) and have `V < τ` (strict, as B3 writes it), whose duration `n × step` is at least `d_min` (with the 1e-9 s tolerance of the other durations). The duration of a run of `n` rows is `n` steps of the uniform grid, the same convention as the valid intervals: the pause is the half-open interval `[t_first, t_last + step)`, so its length is a whole number of steps and it lies inside a valid interval.
2. **Not measured is neither slow nor fast**: such a row ends a run; a pause is never extended or joined across it; a run needs only measured rows (a row whose value is NaN is never below `τ`).
3. **A pause is cut** when the row just before its first row or just after its last row exists in the series and has a pose reason (1 to 6): the pose was lost there and the pause may go on beyond it. The edge of the segment, a row outside the coach's intervals (16), a flagged row (18), the end of the track (22) and a missing scale (19) are boundaries of the protocol or of the track and do not cut. `pause_cut_count` counts the pauses that are cut on either side.
4. **The numbers** (only when `pause_tau` and `pause_min_s` are given): `pause_count`, `pause_total_s` (the sum of the lengths), `pause_longest_s`, `pause_cut_count`; all `uses_threshold`. They are **null when no run of measured rows lasts `d_min`** (a pause could not have been seen, so a 0 would be a 0 that nobody measured, as for `speed_cv`); they are all null with `insufficient_evidence`. A count of 0 is a measured 0.
5. **The threshold is labelled**: `threshold_source = "unsigned_config"` and `record_status = "experimental"` (the model forbids any other combination before P5.09); no event and no pause number without both arguments; no default for either.
6. **`τ` is in the units of the profile** (body lengths per second) and `d_min` in seconds; both finite and above 0 (a `τ` of 0 can never be met by `V < τ`); both or neither, else a `MetricError` naming the arguments, also when no row is measured.

**Constraints that apply (AGENTS.md section 5):** 1 no fabricated observation (a pause lives only on measured rows, nothing is joined or filled; a count that could not be seen is null); 2 real timestamps (the lengths come from the grid's times and its step); 3 layers stay apart (the function reads a series); 4 units documented (Λ/s, s); 5 **no universal threshold** (no default `τ` or `d_min`; the output is `experimental`, labelled `unsigned_config`, never conclusive); 7 no heavy work; 9 nothing personal; 11 no third-party code.

**Unknowns, risks, questions** (answered with the defaults below so that the work can follow; each can be reversed before P5.08 uses it):

- **Q1. Where the detection lives.** Default: **a pure `find_pauses(series, ...)` on a `MetricSeries` and two optional arguments of `group_speed`** that call it, so one call gives profile and pauses and P5.08 re-runs the same call on other layers. Alternative: only a separate function that takes the result of `group_speed` (two steps for every caller).
- **Q2. The interval of a pause.** Default: **`[t_first, t_last + step)`, length `n × step`** (as the valid intervals). Alternative: cells centred on the rows (`[t_first - step/2, t_last + step/2)`), which would not lie inside the valid intervals of P5.01 at the first row and would need a schema change.
- **Q3. The comparisons.** Default: **`V < τ` strict** (B3) and **`n × step >= d_min - 1e-9`** (a pause of exactly `d_min` counts). Alternative: `<=` for `τ` (a speed of exactly 0 is below any `τ > 0`, so only a threshold equal to a measured value differs).
- **Q4. Across a hole.** Default: **never** (convention 2). Alternative: join two stretches separated by a short hole (a new parameter, a number nobody has chosen, and a join would claim a measurement that is missing).
- **Q5. A cut pause.** Default: **counted and reported through `pause_cut_count`**, no schema change. Alternatives: no report (a short pause cut by a hole would look like a short pause); a `censored` field on `MetricEvent` (schema `metric_summary/1` change: not for a count that a statistic already gives).
- **Q6. The numbers.** Default: **count, total, longest, cut count**, null when no run lasts `d_min`. Alternative: also the median length or the share of the segment that is paused (derivable; nobody asked); always numbers (a 0 that nobody measured).
- **Q7. Threshold source.** Default: **`unsigned_config` with `experimental`** until P5.09 supplies signed thresholds; P5.09 will add the signed path (`signed_record` and `enabled`) without changing the detection.
- **Q8. No hysteresis, no merging, no smoothing of `V`.** Default: B3's plain definition. A threshold at the noise floor fragments pauses (measured above): the record's `τ` has to sit above the floor of the footage, which only the pilot (P9, P10) can measure, and a later hysteresis would be a new, named parameter of the record.
- **Q9. Per group.** Default: **pauses of one group at a time** (three metrics, three thresholds); no pause of the whole body.
- **Q10. What T-MET-09 asserts.** Default: SYN-05 and SYN-07 at **noise 0** through the real chain, groups hands and hips, **`τ` = 10 % of the true peak of the group and `d_min` = 0.3 s** (test definitions, not product values): the count equals the number of true slow stretches of at least `d_min` (2 and 1), every boundary within **1 frame (one step of the grid)** of the true crossing of `slow_intervals`; the measured **0.51 to 0.53** steps are recorded. At `τ` = 10 % and `d_min` = 0.2 s the turning point is a pause (3 pauses, 0.23 s) and at 5 % the error is 1.05 to 1.07 steps: both are **recorded and not asserted** as limits (the second against the plan's 1 frame, which holds for a reasonable `τ`).
- **Q11. What T-MET-10 asserts.** Default: **SYN-01** at noise 1 px, three seeds, three groups, `τ` = 0.3 Λ/s (1.7 times the largest floor seen, 0.180) and `d_min` 0.3 s: **exactly one pause**, covering all the measured rows (no fragment from noise); at noise 0 one pause whose `V` is exactly 0. **SYN-06**: at noise 0 the pauses of the hands are **identical** to those of SYN-05 (a hole and a bridge leave no trace); at noise 1 px with `τ` = 0.3 Λ/s the count equals the number of true slow stretches and every reported pause overlaps a true one (no pause in a moving stretch, none from a gap); a hole **inside** a real pause truncates it and the pause is cut (`cut_after`), as measured, with `d_min` 0.1 s for that one case (at 0.3 s the cut piece is below the minimum and the pause is lost without a trace: also recorded). The fragmenting at `τ` = 0.1 is recorded, not asserted.
- **Q12. The parameters echo.** Default: `pause_tau` and `pause_min_s` are **always present** (None when not asked), like `min_valid_s`; the one test of P5.06 that compares the whole dictionary changes in the same commit, and the no-change check against the P5.06 module compares everything else. Alternative: the keys only when asked (a result byte-identical to P5.06's, but a dictionary whose keys vary).
- **Q13. Estimate.** Default 0.75 day (raised from 0.5); or 0.5 day if the generator's `slow_intervals` is left out and each test rebuilds its truth from `speed_at` (not recommended: the truth of T-MET-09 is the point).
- **Q14. A slow stretch that a hole cuts below `d_min`.** A run of slow measured rows that touches a pose-loss row and lasts less than `d_min` may be the end of a longer pause, and today it vanishes with no report (measured: at `d_min` 0.3 s the cut piece of the pause in SYN-06 is lost). Default: **not counted, and written as a limit** (a pause that is seen is a lower bound of what happened, and `pause_cut_count` reports only the pauses that were seen). Alternative: a fifth statistic, `pause_undetermined_count` (slow runs below `d_min` that touch a pose-loss row), which makes the loss visible at the price of one more number and a second result from `find_pauses`. The maintainer may prefer the alternative: it is the honest one if pauses will be judged on footage with holes.
- *Risk:* the count of pauses is very sensitive to `τ` and `d_min` (the turning point appears and merges with a pause as `τ` grows: measured). P5.08 measures the sensitivity to the cut-off; the record decides `τ` and `d_min` from the coach's labels.
- *Risk:* a pause cut by a hole is under-reported (the 0.7 s pause seen as 0.2 s): reported by `pause_cut_count`, not repaired; one cut below `d_min` is not reported at all (Q14).
- *Risk:* at noise levels near the floor the hips of a slow movement have no readable pause (their speed is below the floor): a limit of the footage, shown in section 5.

## 2. Plan

**Design.**

1. `tempo.py` (additive).
   - `Pause` (frozen dataclass): `first_row`, `last_row` (indices into the series), `t_start`, `t_end`, `cut_before`, `cut_after`; property `duration_s = t_end - t_start`.
   - `find_pauses(series: MetricSeries, *, tau: float, min_pause_s: float) -> tuple[Pause, ...]`: validate the series type, `tau` and `min_pause_s` (`_threshold(value, name, units)`: a finite number above 0, not a bool; `MetricError` naming the argument and the units); fewer than two rows (no step) gives `()`; one pass over the rows: a run is the maximal stretch of `reason == 0` and `values < tau`; its length is `n × step_s`; keep it when `>= min_pause_s - 1e-9`; `cut_before` = the row before exists and has a reason 1 to 6, `cut_after` likewise; `t_start = t[first]`, `t_end = t[last] + step_s`. Loop-free is not needed (series are short); the unit tests re-derive the runs with a plain loop and the mutants check the rules.
   - `group_speed(..., pause_tau=None, pause_min_s=None)`: both `None` or both given (`MetricError` naming both otherwise), each checked even when no row is measured; after `assemble_series`, when asked: `pauses = find_pauses(series, ...)`; statistics appended (names, units `count` and `s`, `uses_threshold=True`); the events are the pauses; the parameters echo the two arguments; `make_summary(..., events=..., threshold_source="unsigned_config" if asked else None)`. The pause statistics are null when `why` is not None (no evidence) **or** when no run of measured rows (`series.header.valid_intervals`) lasts `pause_min_s - 1e-9`. The profile (`values`, `reason`, `t_sec`) and the four statistics of P5.06 do not depend on the two arguments.
2. `tests/helpers/synth.py`: `slow_intervals(seq, group, tau)` -> `tuple[(t0, t1), ...]`, the maximal intervals of `[0, duration]` where `speed_at(...)[group] < tau`, found on a 0.2 ms grid with the two ends refined by bisection to 1e-7 s; refuses a scenario with no tempo motion (as `speed_at`) and a `tau` that is not a finite number above 0.
3. Errors: `MetricError` naming the argument: `pause_tau`, `pause_min_s` (not both, not finite, not above 0, a bool, a string), a series that is not a `MetricSeries`.
